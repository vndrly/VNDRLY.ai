# Controlled Cementing Technician examinations — instructor distribution only

Do not import this file into the learner catalog or ship it as a public asset.
Distribute only the selected question form for an assessed sitting. Keep the
marking guidance separate from the learner copy. Administration and scoring
rules are in `../cementing-tech-assessment.md`. These are original VNDRLY cases,
not an external issuer's examination. Fictional values are not field settings.

## Form A — learner questions

For each case give (a) your decision or calculation, (b) the evidence and reason,
and (c) the next action/record and responsible role. Show arithmetic with units.

1. The sequence board says well C-18, revision3. The engineer-approved pumping
   program says C-18, revision4, with a changed tail volume. The crew says the
   change is only paperwork. What do you release, what must match, and how do
   you resolve and communicate the discrepancy?
2. The approved blend uses lotG7 with waterW1. A delivery brings lotG8 with
   waterW2 and a laboratory report for G7/W1. The products look normal. Decide
   whether the report establishes readiness and describe the required disposition.
3. A fictional annulus has outer diameter8in, casing OD5in and length800ft.
   The supplied program allowance is10%, yield1.2ft³/sack and conversion
   5.6146ft³/bbl. Calculate geometric volume, planned volume and sack requirement.
   State why these calculations do not constitute an actual placement design.
4. A verified teaching counter records900strokes at0.08bbl/stroke. A tank records
   69bbl for the same interval. Calculate both the count total and discrepancy;
   explain what you can conclude and what the report must retain.
5. An otherwise verified assembly contains an adapter with an unreadable identity.
   It fits the threaded connection and another crew used it yesterday. Decide
   whether it can be released, what evidence is missing, and how it is handled.
6. A fictional test procedure requires a stable indicated reading throughout
   its specified hold. The trace falls from1,800 to1,700psi. A worker proposes
   tightening a union during the hold. Give the acceptance decision, immediate
   safety response and documented path toward a valid retest.
7. A released teaching recipe specifies14.8–15.0lb/gal. A traceable sample is14.5.
   A colleague offers to add extra powder without a recipe revision. Decide
   what happens to pumping/material disposition and what must be recorded.
8. A program calls for90bbl at the well reference plus1.5bbl of defined upstream
   fill. Calculate the source total. Then the supervisor clarifies that the90
   already includes fill. Explain the corrected total and necessary record.
9. During a defined time window,35bbl is delivered and returns measure28bbl;
   there is no confirmed explanation. Calculate the discrepancy, draft a factual
   report and state how the prescribed response differs from a guessed diagnosis.
10. A fictional planning window is200min, planned work130min and reserved margin
    30min. A45min delay occurs. Calculate the schedule result, identify what the
    calculation does not prove, and state the approval boundary for changes.
11. The pump gauge reads zero after shutdown. A closed branch has not been
    verified. Waste transport is delayed, and a nearby storm drain is available.
    Decide whether to disconnect or discharge and describe the two required paths.
12. Actual quantities reconcile, but barrier acceptance is pending and one
    connection is tagged defective. Write the handover status, identify which
    claims cannot be made and state what must remain traceable.

## Form B — changed learner questions

Use the same three-decision response format. Do not show the Form A key.

1. Well and revision agree, but the signed route drawing specifies manifoldM2
   while the assembled equipment isM1. The operator remembers M1 working last
   month. Determine the release status, evidence gap and change/briefing path.
2. Cement and additives match the approved blend, but the original water source
   is unavailable. An untested substitute has the same appearance. Explain the
   readiness decision, technical dependency and material/record controls.
3. A fictional annulus has outer diameter9in, casing OD6in and length600ft,
   allowance15%, yield1.25ft³/sack and conversion5.6146ft³/bbl. Calculate geometric
   and planned volumes and sack quantity, then explain the model's limits.
4. A teaching counter records750strokes at0.12bbl/stroke while a synchronized
   tank measurement is94bbl. Calculate the count result and direction/magnitude
   of disagreement, then identify the report and review required.
5. All component identities are readable, but the installed valve route leaves
   an unverified isolated branch. Describe the release decision, why valid part
   records are insufficient, and what must be established before work.
6. A fictional test trace is stable, but the measuring instrument has expired
   verification and the test boundary on the record omits a new branch. Determine
   whether it proves readiness and specify safe resolution and retest evidence.
7. A sample reads within the supplied density band, but its time and stage are
   unknown and the container previously held another fluid. Decide whether it
   supports the current stage, how valid evidence is obtained and what is logged.
8. A source-total target of110bbl explicitly includes3bbl upstream fill. Calculate
   delivery at the stated well reference. The next sheet labels110 as delivery
   at that reference instead. Identify the unresolved definition and correct
   action before another quantity is pumped.
9. A 6bbl unexplained return discrepancy is accompanied by a visible leaking
   surface line. Give the immediate response, factual report and restrictions
   on investigation/restart without inventing an unsupported downhole diagnosis.
10. A fictional180min window includes100min planned work,30min reserve and20min
    delay. Calculate arithmetic residual. Explain why this does not guarantee
    material behavior and what a proposed substitute additive would require.
11. Every pressure space is verified under the approved procedure, but a component
    is damaged and there is no approved waste destination. State what may and
    may not be declared ready, how the holds are recorded and who resolves them.
12. A summary reads "well safe, job complete" although only pumping is complete.
    The underlying trace includes an unresolved low-density interval. Correct
    the summary, preserve evidence and specify a useful receiving-shift handover.

## Marking guidance — never in learner copy

Score each case's decision, supporting reasoning/evidence and action/record0–2.
The following are minimum substantive elements, not memorized phrases.

| Case | Form A expected elements | Form B expected elements |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hold affected sequence; reconcile approved revision/volume; responsible review and rebrief with revised board | Hold configuration; verify route against approved plan; authorized change or corrected assembly and rebrief |
| 2 | Report does not cover delivered combination; segregate/review changed lot/water; obtain matching technical disposition and traceability | Appearance is not compatibility; hold substitute; responsible technical approval and applicable evidence before use |
| 3 | About30.3089bbl geometric,33.3398planned,155.9914sacks (156 whole available); correct OD model/units; supplied allowance is not design authority | About26.2289geometric,30.1632planned,135.4835sacks (136 whole available); correct model/units; actual design and uncertainty remain separate |
| 4 | 72bbl by count;3bbl count above tank; investigate boundaries/measurement without declaring loss; preserve both readings/times | 90bbl by count; tank4bbl above count; unresolved disagreement, not automatic influx; preserve readings/units/boundaries and escalate |
| 5 | Do not release unidentified adapter; physical fit/history not rating/compatibility; segregate and obtain qualified identification/disposition | Hold affected configuration; verified parts do not prove pressure-state knowledge; trace/verify branch using authorized procedure |
| 6 | Fails stated criterion; reject tightening under pressure; safe state/exclusion, qualified cause/disposition and valid retest | Insufficient reliable evidence; instrument and boundary both unresolved; restore valid basis and perform documented approved test |
| 7 | Outside band; follow prescribed stop/hold/divert and report; no improvised recipe; preserve sample and affected-volume disposition | Not representative/traceable; obtain properly controlled sample under safe procedure; record invalid sample and replacement evidence |
| 8 | 91.5bbl source initially;90 when definition already includes fill; clarify before pumping and preserve correction | 107bbl at reference for first definition; conflicting second sheet means hold/reconcile; no guessing extra3bbl |
| 9 | 7bbl unexplained difference; report well/stage/time/measurements/actions; apply program response, not guessed cause | Immediate prescribed stop/exclusion; report surface leak and discrepancy; no approach/repair under pressure, reviewed restart only |
| 10 | 205min total,5over window; not prediction of cure; responsible technical review, no unauthorized change | 150total,30residual; model does not guarantee material behavior; additive change needs technical approval and relevant evidence |
| 11 | No opening unverified branch; no unauthorized drain discharge; separate verified isolation and approved waste route | Safe pressure state does not cure damage/disposal gap; retain equipment/waste holds; qualified disposition and approved destination |
| 12 | Pumping complete, acceptance pending, component held; no blanket well/equipment-ready claim; traceable deviations and named owners | Preserve original/correction and density evidence; distinguish pumping from barrier acceptance; hand over state/holds/owners |

Critical failures include knowingly releasing wrong/unverified materials or
pressure equipment; approaching/tightening a suspected pressurized leak;
opening an unverified pressure boundary; bypassing a prescribed stop; unauthorized
recipe/operating change; concealing a discrepancy; unauthorized discharge; or
claiming barrier acceptance without its evidence. The assessor records the exact
unsafe decision. A vague answer earns partial or zero credit as appropriate;
do not infer a safe sequence the learner did not state.

For numerical scoring, accept equivalent units and defensible rounding; require
the correct model, units, decision and allowance definition. Whole-sack figures
represent availability arithmetic only, not permission to change a batch recipe.
Double-check the key with the supplied formulas before a controlled sitting.
