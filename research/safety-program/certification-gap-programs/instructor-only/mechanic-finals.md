# Controlled mechanic examinations — instructor-only

Not a learner lesson or public asset. For a sitting, distribute only its selected
question form and allowed reference cards. Keep this key out of the learner copy.
Use the administration, domain floors and critical-failure rules in
`../mechanic-delivery-assessment.md`. Cases are original and fictional; no value
is an actual equipment setting. Answer each case with decision/calculation,
supporting evidence/reason and next action/record (each0–2).

## M Form A — Field Mechanic shared final

1. A service ticket says "engine bad". The operator reports output dropped only
   after a hose change. What do you conclude, what evidence do you seek and what
   safe diagnostic plan do you document before parts are ordered?
2. Driver power is isolated but an attachment remains hydraulically suspended.
   What prevents maintenance release, what evidence is needed and what procedure
   governs making the work boundary safe?
3. A leaking seal and discolored bearing housing are photographed. The request
   says "clean it first and write normal wear." What evidence should be preserved,
   what can be concluded and what record should accompany further diagnosis?
4. A fictional diameter requirement is19.98–20.00mm. Results are19.99,19.97,20.00.
   Decide conformance, explain whether averaging resolves it and state the next
   measurement/disposition steps.
5. Two removed spacers look identical, but their locations were not recorded.
   One seal face was scratched during removal. Decide what can be reassembled,
   what basis is missing and how the parts should be controlled.
6. A replacement O-ring fits but its material is unknown. The fastener procedure
   assumes dry threads, while the joint is lubricated. Decide readiness and
   specify the required evidence and action for both issues.
7. A fictional alignment card permits0.05mm offset and0.04mm/100mm angular
   deviation. Measurements are0.03 and0.06 respectively. Evaluate each condition,
   explain the acceptance decision and state how correction is verified.
8. An engine temperature alarm occurs immediately after cooling service. A
   colleague proposes opening the hot pressurized cap. Decide the immediate
   response, explain the diagnostic limits and identify a safe authorized path.
9. A fictional proving card limits temperature to75°C. The machine reaches78°C
   but now produces output. Decide release, explain why improvement is or is not
   sufficient and document the next action.
10. The repair was completed but the required proving period was cut short.
    Write the handover status, explain what cannot be claimed and identify
    the follow-up and evidence that remain necessary.

## M Form B — changed shared final

1. Output was low before a recent pump replacement and remains low afterward.
   The mechanic wants to replace it again. State the evidential conclusion,
   competing possibilities and a safe authorized investigation.
2. A raised attachment is on approved verified supports, but an accumulator's
   state has not been established. Decide boundary release and the procedure/
   evidence still needed; explain why the support alone is insufficient.
3. A failed bearing is about to be discarded after the third similar failure.
   Determine evidence retention, limits on root-cause claims and the required
   record/escalation for the repeated pattern.
4. A fictional total-runout limit is0.04mm; measured movement is0.07mm. Calculate
   exceedance, explain setup verification and determine disposition without
   assuming the entire observed movement comes from the shaft.
5. A removed component has no orientation mark and a replacement has damaged
   packaging and uncertain contamination. Determine the assembly holds, required
   evidence and safe parts-control response.
6. Part identity/material match, but the service procedure requires lubricantL1
   and only an unlabeled container is available. Determine readiness, why visual
   resemblance is insufficient and the documented path to correct supply.
7. Fictional limits are0.03mm offset and0.02mm/100mm angle. Measurements0.04 and
   0.01 respectively are recorded. Evaluate both and explain why a flexible
   coupling does not automatically waive the failed condition.
8. A hydraulic code is present and a suspected tiny hose leak is visible. The
   proposal is to feel for it and replace the named component. Give the safety
   response, diagnostic reasoning and required record/specialist boundaries.
9. Temperature meets the supplied test limit, but a new abnormal leak appears.
   Decide acceptance, identify how the test criteria must be applied and specify
   safe investigation and retest documentation.
10. All observed work passed on one mechanical equipment family. The requested
    certificate says "all equipment, electrical and welding included." State
    the defensible award scope, exclusions and evidence retained for the decision.

## P Form A — Pump Mechanic extension

1. A proposed test uses the same discharge-valve maneuver on centrifugal and
   positive-displacement pumps. Determine readiness, the important distinction
   and the approved basis needed before testing.
2. Flow dropped after suction-strainer service. Speed data are reliable, but
   the fitted impeller configuration is unknown. State what can be concluded,
   what comparison is not yet valid and how to investigate safely.
3. A fictional suction card gives available6.2m, required4.5m and mandatory
   margin2.0m. Calculate actual margin and shortfall; state the technical decision
   and why a component swap alone is not established as the solution.
4. A fictional maximum diametral clearance is0.35mm. Mating diameters are60.00
   and60.42mm. Calculate clearance/exceedance and explain why using half the
   difference against that limit is incorrect; state disposition.
5. A new seal fits but its support-service requirement is unknown. The old seal
   failed twice and no alignment record exists. Determine readiness, the missing
   system evidence and the proper repair/proving path.
6. A fictional reciprocating factor is0.050bbl/stroke, count700 and measured
   delivery31bbl. Calculate theoretical delivery and discrepancy; explain the
   diagnosis limits and what must be verified next.
7. A fictional test accepts80–90L/min under its stated conditions. Flow85 passes
   but required seal support is unavailable. Determine release and the evidence
   and action required; explain why flow alone is insufficient.
8. The learner demonstrated centrifugal repair independently and discussed a
   reciprocating repair verbally. Determine the endorsement, evidence limits
   and outstanding requirements for the second family.

## P Form B — changed extension

1. Pump casings look alike but their operating principles and installed protection
   differ. A technician intends to bypass a protection device during diagnosis.
   Determine the response, evidence needed and safe test boundary.
2. The actual impeller is identified, but speed data are invalid and fluid
   conditions changed from the reference curve. Decide whether the curve proves
   damage, what inputs require review and the next safe action.
3. Fictional available5.4m, required4.0m and specified margin1.0m give what
   arithmetic margin? Explain the limited conclusion, remaining assumptions
   and why this calculation is not universal operating approval.
4. Fictional diameters50.00 and50.28mm face maximum diametral clearance0.30mm.
   Evaluate that metric, identify what the result does not establish and state
   the remaining inspection/acceptance requirements.
5. Seal identity/support are correct but bearing condition and assembly settings
   have not been verified. Determine release, the missing evidence and the
   approved path without treating the new seal as a complete repair.
6. A counter gives600strokes at a quoted0.070bbl/stroke, but that factor belongs
   to another liner configuration. Calculate the quoted-model result, explain
   why it cannot diagnose this pump and identify the required correction.
7. A fictional90–100L/min acceptance band produces88L/min, an improvement from80.
   Evaluate release, explain the role of improvement and state proper retest/
   disposition records.
8. A reciprocating component was replaced with continuous instructor prompting.
   The learner has not completed an independent proving route. Determine whether
   the operational endorsement is earned and the required reassessment/evidence.

## Instructor marking key

Each row supplies the three substantive decisions to score. Accept equivalent
safe wording and reasoning; do not award points for a bare "ask supervisor"
without identifying the problem, evidence and necessary boundary.

| Case | Form A | Form B |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | Symptom not diagnosis; inspect history/configuration and alternative causes; safe authorized test plan | Repeat replacement unsupported; investigate system/measurement/configuration; preserve tests and scope |
| M2 | Unsupported suspended load prevents release; approved support and complete boundary verification; existing authorized procedure | Accumulator unresolved; apply prescribed stored-energy verification; support controls a different hazard |
| M3 | Preserve both observations; no unsupported normal-wear claim; investigate support/system with traceable record | Retain failed part/history; no proven cause from repetition alone; qualified review and documented hypotheses |
| M4 | 19.97 fails; average cannot cure local failure; verify method and obtain disposition | 0.03mm exceeds; verify fixture/instrument/setup; record and obtain disposition without guessed cause |
| M5 | Hold uncertain assembly and damaged seal; recover correct location/procedure; segregate and document | Hold both orientation and condition uncertainty; use approved assembly basis; segregate questionable component |
| M6 | Both unresolved; verify material/service compatibility and fastening conditions; no guessed substitution/torque | Unknown lubricant cannot be accepted; identity matters; obtain approved product and preserve hold |
| M7 | Offset passes, angle fails by0.02; all criteria required; correct under procedure and remeasure | Offset fails by0.01, angle passes; coupling flexibility not a waiver; correct cause and document recheck |
| M8 | No hot-cap opening; safe stop under procedure; diagnose with history/authorized tests, not alarm-only replacement | No hand leak search; follow safe stop/isolation; code is evidence, not complete diagnosis; respect test scope |

Critical failures: working in an unverified energy boundary; unsupported loads;
live leak probing; unsafe pressure/hot-fluid exposure; disabling required guards
or protective functions; knowingly unsuitable parts or unauthorized pressure
repair; falsifying findings or issuing an unobserved scope. Apply the packet's
failure rules even when the total score would otherwise pass.

Numerical results use the supplied fictional definitions. Credit the correct
units/model and decision, not only a final number. Assessors verify this key
against the actual distributed form before use. Retain failed and changed
attempts separately with reasons, remediation and assessor identity.
