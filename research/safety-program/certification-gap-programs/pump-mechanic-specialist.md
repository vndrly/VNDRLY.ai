# Pump Mechanic — pump-specific course under development

Use the single shared mechanical-maintenance course M01–M10 and existing VUC019
hazardous-energy courses. Do not duplicate their instruction here. This course
adds pump-specific interpretation, diagnosis, component service and proving.
It remains outside the catalog until its complete program is reviewed and usable.

The intended credential scope must identify centrifugal and/or reciprocating
pump families actually assessed. It does not confer pressure-boundary welding,
electrical installation, hazardous-area certification, engineering redesign or
competence on every pump model. Required equipment manuals, service instructions
and employer authorization are part of delivery. Numerical examples below are
fictional and do not establish actual operating limits.

## P01 — Identify the pump principle before diagnosing the symptom

A centrifugal pump transfers energy through a rotating impeller and converts
that energy into pressure/flow within its hydraulic design. Its operating point
depends on the pump characteristics and system resistance. A positive-displacement
pump moves a defined volume through its displacement cycle; the system determines
the pressure needed to move that fluid, subject to equipment and protection limits.
Do not apply a centrifugal throttling assumption to a positive-displacement
machine. Identify the exact pump type and protection arrangement before a test.

Read the nameplate, configuration and relevant performance information. Identify
driver speed, impeller or displacement configuration, direction, fluid properties,
service conditions and installed protective devices. A capacity number without
its speed, fluid and configuration does not define the actual duty. Confirm that
the current machine matches the documents before using a chart or spare-parts list.

**Worked case:** two units share a similar casing size, but one is centrifugal
and the other reciprocating. A mechanic proposes the same discharge-control test
for both. Hold the proposed test and establish the correct machine-specific
procedure and protection basis. **Check:** Why is a closed discharge especially
important to identify on a positive-displacement system? **Answer:** Displacement
continues to demand a flow path; pressure can increase dangerously without the
appropriate protection and operating controls. Never defeat those protections
to prove a diagnosis. **Remediation:** draw both systems with their actual
approved control/protection functions, without inventing a relief setting.

## P02 — Separate a pump problem from a system problem

Gather suction and discharge conditions, flow, speed, fluid properties, relevant
valve positions, instrument health and recent changes. Compare like conditions
with the approved baseline. A reduction in flow can arise from the system,
the pump, measurement error or a combination. Trace whether a restriction,
air ingress, supply problem, changed fluid or configuration could explain the
symptom before ordering a replacement rotating assembly.

Use the actual performance curve and operating range for the configured pump.
Do not extrapolate beyond its approved range or transfer a water curve to a
different service without the proper technical treatment. If the unit is not
safe to run, use existing records and isolated inspection rather than inducing
an unsafe test. The shared maintenance course supplies the evidence and test
planning method; this lesson applies it to the hydraulic system.

**Worked case:** flow fell after an upstream strainer service, while the driver
speed indication is unchanged. Investigate supply, strainer installation and
measurement validity through the authorized process before concluding impeller
wear. **Changed case:** no upstream change is found but speed data are unreliable.
The missing speed evidence still prevents a confident curve comparison.
**Check:** Does a low-flow complaint prove internal pump damage? **Answer:** No.
**Remediation:** list three competing explanations and an authorized observation
that would distinguish each, including what cannot safely be tested while running.

## P03 — Understand suction limitations and cavitation evidence

Cavitation involves local vapor formation and collapse when pressure conditions
permit it. It can damage components and affect performance, but noise alone is
not a complete diagnosis. Air entrainment, mechanical contact and other faults
can also produce abnormal sound or vibration. Examine suction-system conditions,
fluid temperature/properties, restrictions, supply level and the actual pump
requirements with the responsible technical person.

Net positive suction head available and required must be compared on a consistent
basis with the specified margin; a mechanic must not invent that margin. For a
fictional reviewed teaching card, availableNPSH5.0m and required3.8m leave1.2m
arithmetic difference. If the card requires1.5m margin, the condition fails that
card by0.3m. Do not conclude that simply being above requiredNPSH proves reliable
operation. Actual limits and uncertainty govern the decision.

**Changed case:** a process change reduces availableNPSH to4.2m while required
remains3.8m. Only0.4m remains; replacing the impeller does not fix the supplied
system deficiency by itself. Escalate the system condition rather than modifying
equipment without approval. **Check:** Can a suction restriction be dismissed
because discharge pressure is low? **Answer:** No; the whole operating condition
must be examined. **Remediation:** annotate the supply-to-pump path and identify
which inputs require verified measurement or engineering determination.

## P04 — Inspect wear and clearances without assuming a universal tolerance

After verified isolation and approved disassembly, inspect the impeller or
displacement components, wear surfaces, shaft/sleeve, bearings, seals, valves
and casing as applicable to the identified pump. Record erosion, corrosion,
contact marks, cracking indications, deposits and dimensional changes. Follow
the correct examination/disposition process; a visual mark does not authorize
grinding, welding or blending a pressure boundary.

Measure the specified features at the prescribed locations with suitable
instruments. The shared maintenance course teaches measurement control. Apply
the actual pump's dimensional definitions: diametral and radial clearances are
not interchangeable, and assembly position can affect the result. Do not compare
a radial measurement to a diametral limit and declare a convenient pass.

Fictional card P-C defines maximum *diametral* clearance0.40mm. Measured mating
diameters75.00 and75.46mm give0.46mm diametral difference, exceeding the card
by0.06mm. Half that difference is radial clearance0.23mm, but comparing0.23 to
the diametral0.40limit would be invalid. **Check:** What happens after a confirmed
out-of-tolerance result? **Answer:** Record it and obtain approved repair or
replacement disposition; do not alter a tolerance or relabel the measurement.
**Remediation:** repeat the calculation with a changed diameter pair and explain
the measurement definition to the assessor.

## P05 — Service seals and bearings as parts of a system

Identify the actual seal arrangement and support requirements. A mechanical
seal, packing arrangement and different seal support plans do not share one
installation or startup procedure. Verify component identity, material/service
compatibility, condition, setting method and cleanliness. Protect precision
surfaces and follow the current manufacturer instructions for the particular
assembly. A generic course must not invent a universal seal-setting dimension.

Inspect the support conditions that can cause repeat failure: shaft condition,
alignment, bearings, vibration, fluid state and required support/flush paths.
Replacing a leaking part without checking those conditions can repeat the failure.
Do not run a unit with unverified seal support to see whether the new seal lasts.
Use the actual procedure's preparation and proving requirements.

For bearings, establish the correct type, fit, orientation, handling method,
lubrication and condition. Use approved installation tools and methods, not
uncontrolled blows through rolling elements. Do not assume every bearing is
grease-packed or that additional lubricant is always beneficial. Preserve a
failed bearing for analysis when the repair plan requires it.

**Worked case:** the replacement seal matches dimensions but its material and
support-plan requirements are unknown. Hold installation/release until verified.
**Check:** Why record bearing and alignment evidence during a seal repair?
**Answer:** They can reveal system conditions responsible for the seal problem.
**Remediation:** write a repair plan that tests the suspected cause as well as
replacing the failed component.

## P06 — Reciprocating-pump fluid end and drive interfaces

Identify the exact reciprocating design, cylinder/liner or plunger arrangement,
valve assemblies, packing, lubrication, drive and protection systems. Follow
model-specific inspection and service instructions. A pressure rating attached
to one component is not permission to use the assembled system at that pressure;
the installed configuration and service conditions govern.

Inspect the fluid end and service components for the specified wear, damage and
fit criteria after verified safe isolation. Maintain parts traceability and
orientation; an apparently interchangeable valve or packing set can have
different service requirements. Cracks or pressure-boundary damage require
qualified disposition, not a field cosmetic repair. The mechanic must not
defeat an interlock or relief arrangement to obtain more output.

For a fictional volume-accounting model with effective displacement0.060bbl per
stroke,800strokes correspond to48bbl. If the independently defined measured
delivery is42bbl, the6bbl difference is evidence to investigate; it does not alone
prove a specific valve has failed. Confirm the measurement window, applicable
factor, supply conditions and authorized diagnostic process.

**Changed case:** the factor belongs to a different liner configuration. Correct
the calculation basis before diagnosing efficiency. **Check:** Can a pressure
boundary be repaired with an unapproved weld to finish the shift? **Answer:** No.
**Remediation:** prepare a hold/disposition record identifying the affected
component, evidence, restricted scope and qualified decision owner.

## P07 — Recommission with an approved performance test

Before release verify assembly, clearances, alignment, lubrication, seal/support
services, connections, guards, instruments and protection functions according
to the actual pump procedure. Confirm the system is ready for the intended
fluid and that startup conditions are correct for that pump type. Do not
assume every pump can be started dry or against the same valve arrangement.

The test plan specifies the operating points, measurements, limits, observation
period and abort criteria. Establish communication with the operator and keep
personnel clear of rotating and pressure exposure. Record actual pressure,
flow, speed, temperature, vibration and leakage information where required by
the plan. A smooth-sounding unit is not a complete acceptance test.

Fictional test card P-T accepts flow95–105L/min at its stated conditions and
requires all its other conditions satisfied. Measured98L/min passes that one
metric. A simultaneously failed seal-support check still prevents release.
In a changed case measured92L/min is outside the card, even if the old unit
performed worse. Improvement is not the same as meeting acceptance criteria.

**Check:** What if the plan contains no acceptance criteria for a required
measurement? **Answer:** Resolve the missing test basis with the responsible
authority before claiming a pass. **Remediation:** construct a complete mock
test report that separates each criterion and its evidence.

## P08 — Close the pump repair and define the endorsement

Record the exact pump family/model, service, configuration, symptoms, findings,
parts, dimensions, assembly settings, test conditions and results. State whether
a cause was established or remains a hypothesis. Identify any follow-up test,
monitoring or system investigation and its owner. Preserve original failed
results and the changed retest evidence instead of replacing them with only
a final green status.

The assessment must identify whether centrifugal, reciprocating or both routes
were demonstrated. A pass on a benign centrifugal training rig is not evidence
of fluid-end service on a high-pressure reciprocating pump. A written explanation
can demonstrate knowledge of an excluded route, but not its practical competence.
The credential records the actual assessed scope and employer authorization
remains separate.

**Worked case:** a learner passes all theory and centrifugal practical tasks but
has not worked on the reciprocating trainer. The reciprocating endorsement is
not awarded. **Changed case:** the learner replaces a reciprocating valve under
coaching but cannot independently verify the correct configuration. More practice
and independent reassessment are needed; attendance is insufficient.
**Check:** Is "pump mechanic—unlimited" a defensible award from one machine?
**Answer:** No. State supported families/tasks and limitations.
**Remediation:** draft an honest endorsement record using only observed evidence.
