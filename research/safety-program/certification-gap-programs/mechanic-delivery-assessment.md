# Field Mechanic and Pump Mechanic — shared delivery and assessment

Development-only material. Two proposed certification pathways reuse one
maintenance course and the existing catalog prerequisites. This document does
not create duplicate learner courses, company library records or awards.

## Program pathways

**Field Mechanic:** M01–M10 in `shared-mechanical-maintenance.md`, existing VUC019
hazardous-energy courses and applicable shared safety courses, all M applications,
M practical stations, controlled M final and an observed equipment-specific
maintenance route. The award states the mechanical equipment/tasks demonstrated.
It excludes engine rebuild, electrical qualification, welding, lifting-equipment
inspection, engineered modification or other specialty not separately assessed.

**Pump Mechanic:** the same M course and prerequisites, plus P01–P08 in
`pump-mechanic-specialist.md`, P applications, the controlled P final and the
relevant centrifugal and/or reciprocating practical route. Completed unchanged
M material is reused; taking the second pathway does not create another M course
or require a duplicate M completion solely because the bundle changed.

Entry includes employer-approved mechanical experience/placement, basic unit
arithmetic, schematic reading and the existing energy-control competency.
Assess those prerequisites diagnostically and teach missing skills before
practice. Instructors and assessors must have demonstrated competence for the
specific task/equipment, not just a general training title. Record exact scope.

## Teaching sequence

Use an actual applicable manual, deenergized training assemblies, sample service
records and current approved procedures. Teach the ten M domains in order,
working through each example before its check. Plan two teaching/practice days
for M plus sufficient individually observed equipment work. The P extension
requires its own technical instruction and additional route practice; plan a
further day for theory/workshops, extending for experience and equipment needs.
These are scheduling estimates, not guaranteed mastery or mandated legal hours.

Teach only within the instructor's competence and the facility's approved
conditions. Use inert examples and tabletop defects; do not create live leaks,
remove guards from running machines, disable protective devices or introduce
real contamination to make an exercise memorable. Actual pressure, electrical,
lifting and running tests require their separately authorized safe arrangements.

## Guided M applications with explained outcomes

1. **Complaint reconstruction (M01):** give "weak pump" and a record showing a
   recent hose change. The learner writes observed symptom, conditions, timing,
   hypotheses and a safe test. Correct response investigates configuration/supply
   and evidence before condemning the pump; it does not assume the hose is guilty.
2. **Energy/load diagram (M02):** on a deenergized model, mark electrical drive,
   raised load and accumulator paths. Correct response applies the existing
   equipment-specific isolation/support procedure to all, not just the disconnect.
3. **As-found record (M03):** use photos of a leak and hot bearing indication.
   Record both observations and uncertainties before proposing a seal replacement.
   The report must preserve evidence and avoid a fabricated root cause.
4. **Dimensional decisions (M04):** use24.98–25.00mm limits with24.99/24.97/24.99.
   The24.97 location fails. A favorable average does not satisfy the feature
   requirement. Runout0.08 against0.05 fails by0.03, subject to verified setup.
5. **Removal/parts control (M05):** arrange labeled mock spacers, a damaged seal
   and an unknown-orientation component. Segregate the damaged item and recover
   the assembly basis before proceeding; resemblance does not prove interchangeability.
6. **Parts/fluids/fasteners (M06):** supply correct-size but unknown-material seal,
   two lubricant labels and incompatible dry/lubricated torque instructions.
   Correct response holds each unresolved item and names the required approved
   source; there is no guessed universal torque or lubricant substitution.
7. **Alignment (M07):** use fictional offset0.06 against0.04mm and angle0.02
   against0.03mm/100mm. Offset fails, angle passes. Correct cause, remeasure
   and keep the full record; restoring only the passing dimension is insufficient.
8. **Diagnostic boundaries (M08):** introduce a hydraulic complaint and an engine
   temperature alarm after service. The learner chooses authorized safe tests,
   avoids live leak probing/hot-cap opening and separates codes from diagnosis.
9. **Proving (M09):** give83°C against a supplied80°C limit, then75°C with an
   abnormal leak. Neither is accepted under the stated criteria. Demonstrate
   safe stop, record and technical disposition rather than "better than before."
10. **Closeout (M10):** rewrite "seal replaced, ready" where alignment was not
    measured and the proving run is incomplete. Correct record distinguishes
    completed action, unknown cause and pending acceptance evidence.

## Guided P applications with explained outcomes

1. **Type and controls (P01):** compare identified centrifugal and reciprocating
   diagrams. Require the actual model-specific startup/control/protection basis.
   A common casing appearance does not justify a common discharge test.
2. **System diagnosis (P02):** low flow follows strainer service. Compare valid
   speed/supply/configuration evidence with the correct curve before attributing
   wear. Invalid speed data leave a missing input rather than a convenient answer.
3. **Suction margin (P03):** fictional5.0available minus3.8required gives1.2m,
   below the supplied1.5m margin by0.3. At4.2available the margin is0.4m.
   The solution identifies a system/design review; it does not invent a setting.
4. **Clearance (P04):**75.46minus75.00 is0.46mm diametral, outside0.40 by0.06.
   Radial0.23 is not compared with a diametral limit. Retain the actual definition.
5. **Seal/support (P05):** a matching-size seal has unknown material/support
   requirements. Hold and verify; replacing it does not resolve unknown shaft,
   bearing or alignment conditions.
6. **Reciprocating model (P06):**800strokes times0.060 gives48bbl; measured42
   leaves6bbl unexplained. Verify factor/configuration and measurement boundaries
   before naming a failed valve. Never defeat protection or weld an unapproved repair.
7. **Performance (P07):**98L/min meets the fictional95–105 range, but failed
   support readiness still prevents release. At92L/min the flow criterion itself
   fails. All applicable acceptance conditions must be satisfied.
8. **Scope (P08):** centrifugal-only observation cannot earn a reciprocating
   endorsement. Coaching during a task is practice, not an independent pass.
   Write scope and remaining evidence explicitly.

## Practical equipment and station criteria

Provide the actual approved manuals and repair/test plans, deenergized assemblies,
appropriate instruments with verified status, permitted tools/fixtures, labeled
parts, safe handling supports, guard arrangements, fluid containment and blank
records. For operating demonstrations provide a controlled authorized trainer
or operation, an emergency plan and a competent person able to stop the task.
Do not assume an equipment-specific procedure is safe solely because it is printed.

Score each station on five dimensions0–2: evidence/preparation, technical
correctness, safe sequencing, communication and traceable records. Two means
independent correct performance with reason; one safe but incomplete/prompted;
zero missing/incorrect/unsafe. Every station requires8/10 or better, no zero,
and no critical failure. Preserve each attempt. Stop an unsafe attempt immediately,
teach/practice the gap, then use a changed independent reassessment.

| Station | Observable requirements | Critical failures |
|---|---|---|
| M-A Diagnosis | Identify asset/symptom; retain conditions; propose competing hypotheses and authorized test; document scope | Unsafe reproduction; unqualified test; invented observation |

## Integrated routes and award

Field Mechanic requires an individual end-to-end maintenance job on the stated
equipment family: complaint review, approved diagnosis, verified boundary,
inspection/measurement, authorized service, reassembly, testing and handover.
The assessor records the actual equipment and procedures. Tabletop evidence
alone is not an operational qualification. Exclude work outside the approved
mechanical scope rather than quietly treating a verbal discussion as practice.

Pump Mechanic requires the common route plus an integrated pump repair and
proving route for each claimed family. Centrifugal and reciprocating endorsements
are independent. If a training setup cannot safely demonstrate a required task,
that task remains pending. Do not introduce a live hazardous defect for assessment.

The M written final has ten cases with three scored decisions each:60points,
pass48overall and4/6 in every case, with no critical unsafe decision. The P final
adds eight cases:48points, pass39overall and4/6 per case, with no critical unsafe
decision. Allow90minutes for M and75 for P, calculator and provided reference
cards only. The separate instructor-only forms/keys must be available and reviewed
before this packet can be accepted complete. Changed retakes follow targeted
remediation; an identical memorized paper is not an adequate reassessment.

Award requires all specified shared-course evidence, corrected lesson checks,
applications, passed written domains, passed practical stations and the applicable
integrated route. A study record is appropriate while practical evidence is
pending; do not issue the full credential conditionally as though the work were
demonstrated. Employer appointment/authorization and legally required specialty
credentials remain separate. Reassess material equipment/task changes or observed
deficiencies. An annual evidence-review reminder is VNDRLY administrative policy,
not a fabricated legal expiry or attendance-only renewal.
