# VNDRLY/U Offshore Muster Checker — Workplace Competence

VUC102 • Original materials • 18 November 2026

Scope: identity-based accounting at an assigned offshore muster point, reporting and transfer under the installation emergency plan. Not OPITO approval, a coordinator qualification or authority to command searches/evacuation. Full proprietary criteria were unavailable; public source scope informs this original program.

Entry to this VNDRLY workplace route requires local induction, role familiarization and safe participation in approved drills. The public external product lists no formal entry requirements; local safety preparation is not waived by that fact. Planned learning/review allocation 24 hours: 8 instruction, 4 labs, 4 guided practice, 6 observed workplace-drill evidence/review, 2 written assessment. Workplace scheduling may require longer.

Objectives: prepare current records/equipment, reach an authorized safe point, verify identities, reconcile discrepancies, report accurately, assist orderly muster, preserve identity during movement and hand over reliably.

## Q102-01 — Role, authority and workplace readiness

A muster checker establishes who is physically present at an assigned muster point, records reliable identity evidence and reports discrepancies to the muster coordinator. The checker does not independently declare the whole installation accounted for, order a search or authorize evacuation. Learn the installation's alarm meanings, assigned point, alternate point, communications, relief arrangements and escalation path. A local role card must identify who receives reports and who can direct relocation.

Before duty, verify the current roster source, revision and time, working radio or approved alternative, forms, writing materials and any electronic reader. Know how to continue if power or network access fails. Confirm the arrangements for visitors, contractors, response personnel, people receiving assistance and those at medical facilities. A person absent from your point may be elsewhere under an authorized arrangement, but that must be confirmed rather than assumed.

Worked case: the checker receives an older printed list and a newer electronic update adding a contractor. Retain the versions, establish the authorized current baseline and tell the coordinator about any unresolved discrepancy. Do not discard the contractor because the person does not fit the old list. Workplace competence requires observed performance in approved local drills, not only this reading.

## Q102-02 — Responding to an alarm and reaching the assigned point

On alarm, follow the installation's emergency instructions and use the safe designated route. Collect only the role equipment the plan requires and permits; do not delay to retrieve personal property or a missing device from an unsafe area. Assess route information and report a blocked or threatened path. An alternate muster point is used according to the plan and command instruction, not chosen silently because it is convenient.

At the point, identify yourself, establish communication with the coordinator and report readiness or limitations. Keep access and escape paths clear. Organize arrivals so identity checking does not create a dangerous queue. Instructions should be short and understandable in noise or stress. If an unsafe condition threatens the point, report it immediately and follow protective relocation arrangements; accurate paperwork never requires remaining in danger.

Worked case: the main route is closed by a drill card. The checker must use the prebriefed approved alternate, report the change and establish where people are being received. Continuing to the original point through the marked hazard to preserve the expected count is a failure, even if the register is later correct.

## Q102-03 — Verifying identity and recording presence

Record a person as present only using the approved verification method. This may combine direct observation, name/identifier confirmation and an authorized electronic system. A scanned card without its holder is not proof that the person is at muster. Similar names, shared surnames, nicknames and borrowed badges require resolution through the approved identity process. Do not guess to keep the line moving.

Separate arrival time, verified presence and other reported locations. Mark entries clearly and preserve corrections. An electronic check can assist but must not erase uncertainty from a mismatch or failed device. If a person is not on the expected list, keep them safe, record the identity as unlisted/unreconciled and notify the coordinator. Do not send an unlisted person away merely because the software has no matching row.

Worked case: two workers named Sam Lee have different identifiers S14 and S41. A verbal Sam is here cannot mark both present. Confirm each individual identity and report the remaining uncertainty. If one person carries both cards, the second card remains unverified; duplicate credentials do not create a second person.

## Q102-04 — Reconciling duplicates, missing people and roster changes

Use identity-based reconciliation. Compare the authorized baseline with verified arrivals and authorized location reports, retaining the distinction between what your point confirms and what the coordinator confirms elsewhere. Remove duplicate counting without deleting the evidence that produced the discrepancy. A complete numerical total can still be wrong when one identity appears twice and another is missing.

Report missing or unconfirmed identities promptly with last-known information and its source/time. Do not conduct an independent search or ask another attendee to go back into the installation. Command uses the information to decide the response. If a person later arrives or is verified elsewhere, issue a correction with time and source so the coordinator can update the installation picture. Baseline changes must be authorized and traceable; a verbal claim that someone left before the alarm is not enough to silently remove them.

Worked case: expected IDs A01–A10; received list A01–A08, A08, A09. There are ten entries but nine unique people; A10 is unconfirmed. Report nine locally verified, one duplicate and A10 unresolved. If the coordinator later confirms A10 with the medic, record that as coordinator-confirmed elsewhere, not physically present at your point.

## Q102-05 — Communicating counts and discrepancies

Use the prescribed report format: point, time, roster version, expected identities or count, verified local count, unlisted people, duplicates, missing/unconfirmed identities, hazards and assistance needs. State what is known and what is still being checked. Obtain acknowledgment and correct a wrong readback. Keep records of both original and updated reports so a late correction does not appear to be a new person.

If communication fails, use the approved fallback and verify receipt. Do not assume a message sent through a device reached the coordinator. Avoid broadcasting unnecessary medical or personal details on an open channel; provide the operational information and use the approved private route for sensitive details. Maintain updates when the situation changes, not only at the initial count.

Worked case: the checker reports nine at Point A, but the coordinator reads nineteen. Correct the number and point immediately using the agreed clear phrasing, then obtain the correct readback. A hurried yes can add ten imaginary people to the emergency picture.

## Q102-06 — Maintaining an orderly and accessible muster

People may arrive frightened, confused, injured or unfamiliar with the location. Give calm, concise directions and explain that they must remain available for further instructions. Keep walkways clear and do not physically restrain people. Use the installation's arrangements for language, hearing, mobility or other assistance needs. Ask for the designated assistance resource; do not improvise a lift or medical treatment beyond training.

Monitor the point's conditions and attendance after the initial count. Someone who leaves for medical care, a response role or relocation must be recorded and reported under the plan. A count taken once becomes stale when people move. Report deterioration such as smoke, crowding or a blocked exit. Follow command directions and the local emergency plan if the point must be cleared.

Worked case: a person needs assistance and the assigned helper is absent. Tell the coordinator immediately and request the approved replacement arrangement. Do not mark the assistance complete because it is written on the plan. Keep the person informed and preserve safe access while the response is arranged.

## Q102-07 — Relocation and evacuation handover

Relocation creates a risk of losing or double-counting people between points. Before movement, follow the authorized instruction and verify the destination, route, responsible receiving role and communications. Record the identities transferred, departure time and receiving acknowledgment. Reconcile the receiving list with the outgoing list; a total alone cannot reveal a substitution or duplicate.

The checker supports orderly movement but does not override the responsible transport operator's capacity or safety requirements. Do not mark people evacuated when they are only queued, assigned seats or listed on a plan. Distinguish departed, received and unconfirmed transit status. If an individual cannot be accounted for at destination, report the discrepancy immediately using last-known facts without launching an unauthorized search.

Worked case: eight people leave Point A for B. B confirms seven named identities and one duplicate. Seven unique arrivals are confirmed, not eight. Retain the missing identity and transfer time and notify the coordinator. Sending another person to fill the numerical gap would not resolve the original person's location.

## Q102-08 — Degraded systems, relief and evidence

A failed reader, network or printer does not end the duty to account for people. Use the approved paper fallback, current available roster and explicit uncertainty reporting. Reconcile paper and electronic records when service returns; do not import duplicates or overwrite the original event times. Record which source was used and when. Device recovery is not proof that missed arrivals were captured.

During relief, transfer the point status, roster version, local identities, people confirmed elsewhere, unresolved discrepancies, movements, hazards and unacknowledged reports. The relief checker reads back critical items and accepts the duty. Preserve evidence for the drill review with appropriate privacy controls. An assessment needs actual observation and traceable records; fabricated times or retrospective signatures cannot replace them.

Worked case: a reader recovers and uploads the same six arrivals already recorded on paper. Match identities and retain a single presence record with linked sources. Do not add the two sets and report twelve. Tell the coordinator how the reconciliation was performed and what remains uncertain.

## Q102-09 — Practice, assessment and maintaining competence

Competence is demonstrated in the role during approved workplace drills with the actual local arrangements. The assessor observes preparation, route response, identity verification, reconciliation, communications, assistance, transfer and relief. Synthetic exercises help teach changed conditions but cannot establish that a person can operate the actual workplace system. No live emergency is required or deliberately created.

After each drill, compare the log with observed actions and coordinator acknowledgments. Identify errors in both individual performance and the system: stale rosters, unclear point labels, poor radio coverage or inaccessible instructions. Correct the root cause, practice a changed situation and reassess without coaching. Keep a record of what was observed rather than awarding competence for attendance.

Worked case: a learner passes the written questions but has not demonstrated paper fallback or a relocation handover at the workplace. Those evidence gaps remain open. Arrange approved drills that cover them. The full workplace certificate cannot be issued merely because the coursebook and quiz are complete.

# VUC102 workplace assessment workbook

## Resources, supervision and safe adaptation

Use the actual installation's approved drill arrangements, current muster point/alternate, role card, roster system, radio/fallback, forms and assistance procedures. The duty holder approves timing, routes and operational interfaces. A competent muster-role assessor observes; a drill controller coordinates role players and stops unsafe activity. Do not trigger an unannounced alarm, obstruct an actual escape route, simulate a missing person by hiding someone in danger, or contact real emergency services. Faults and hazards are cards or isolated training-system states. Real emergency instructions override the exercise immediately.

For classroom practice use synthetic identities, mock cards/reader, paper lists, radios on a training channel and a marked safe room. No real personal data is required. Practice can be fully simulated, but the workplace award requires two observed approved workplace drills W1/W2 using the actual local system/arrangements and changed conditions. An actual emergency is not required. If workplace access is unavailable, record theory/simulation only and leave the workplace decision open.

## Workplace evidence W1 and W2

W1 must observe preparation, safe point establishment, actual identity checking, count/report, acknowledgment and relief during an approved workplace drill. W2 changes at least three conditions and must additionally cover device/communication fallback, a roster discrepancy, assistance coordination and a named relocation/receipt exercise. Use cards for dangerous/rare conditions; actual participants follow safe approved drill movements only. If a function cannot be demonstrated safely at the site, document the gap and arrange an approved equivalent workplace exercise—not a fabricated observation.

For each drill retain site/date/ERP version, roster revision or privacy-safe trace, observer notes, report/readback times, discrepancy/correction trail, transfer record and debrief. Competent witness statements supplement but do not replace the assessor's direct observation of critical decisions. Two dates or distinct approved drill events are required by this original package. The public external specification's exact evidence rules were unavailable; this is not an assertion of its prescribed drill count.

## Assessment and credential rules

Nine dimensions scored 0–4: 0 unsafe/absent; 1 major help; 2 safe but incomplete/prompted; 3 independent accurate traceable; 4 independent with timely anticipation. Require 29/36, each at least 3, no critical error, all functions covered across W1/W2 with critical identity/report/transfer actions satisfactory independently. Critical errors: false presence/all-accounted report, deliberate duplicate counting, unsafe route/search instruction, unapproved overload or handling, fabricated evidence, or concealed missing transfer identity. Freeze unsafe simulation, preserve evidence, remediate and use changed observation. Do not average a critical failure into a pass.

Complete 18 formative checks with corrected reasoning and 4 labs. Controlled final: 9 cases, 27 decisions, 54 points, pass 44 and at least 4/6 each, no critical error, 90 minutes. Private changed retake and keys remain outside learner package. Assessors give formative feedback, then obtain unprompted changed evidence. Second competent reviewer resolves disputed decisions using the trace, not reduced criteria.

Only after knowledge and authentic workplace evidence pass issue VNDRLY/U Offshore Muster Checker — Workplace Assessed, with learner/site, scope, package hash, drill dates, assessor and limitations. If only simulated practice exists, label the result theory/simulation and do not use workplace-assessed wording. No OPITO certificate or appointment to coordinator/commander follows. Set a two-year VNDRLY review/renewal date and require earlier review after material change or performance concern; continued local drills remain necessary. The public OPITO product also lists two-year validity, but this certificate is independent. Materials completion is not evidence of any learner's workplace completion.

# VUC102 formative checks

Attempt before reading the explanation. Correct errors and revisit the matching chapter/station. These are not the controlled final.

## Q102-01-L1

May a checker declare the entire installation accounted for?

**Answer:** No. Report the assigned-point evidence to the coordinator.

**Reason/remediation:** Local observation has a limited scope. Revisit Q102-01 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-01-L2

An old roster omits a newly authorized contractor. What next?

**Answer:** Establish the current authorized baseline and retain/report the discrepancy.

**Reason/remediation:** An outdated list is not authority to exclude someone. Revisit Q102-01 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-02-L1

A designated route is blocked. Continue to preserve the planned count?

**Answer:** No. Use the approved alternate arrangements and report the change.

**Reason/remediation:** Accounting does not justify exposure. Revisit Q102-02 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-02-L2

Should missing equipment be retrieved from an unsafe area?

**Answer:** No. Follow the emergency plan and report the limitation.

**Reason/remediation:** Role equipment does not outrank life protection. Revisit Q102-02 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-03-L1

One person carries two badges. Are two people present?

**Answer:** No. Verify each actual individual.

**Reason/remediation:** A credential scan is not a second human. Revisit Q102-03 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-03-L2

An unlisted person arrives. Send them away?

**Answer:** No. Keep them safe, record unlisted/unreconciled identity and notify the coordinator.

**Reason/remediation:** The roster discrepancy must be resolved without exposing the person. Revisit Q102-03 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-04-L1

A01–A08, A08, A09 against A01–A10: count and gap?

**Answer:** Nine unique; A10 unconfirmed.

**Reason/remediation:** Ten entries include a duplicate. Revisit Q102-04 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-04-L2

A10 is confirmed by the coordinator at medical. Mark locally present?

**Answer:** No. Record confirmed elsewhere with source/time.

**Reason/remediation:** Location categories must remain accurate. Revisit Q102-04 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-05-L1

Nine is read back as nineteen. What next?

**Answer:** Correct point/count and obtain accurate readback.

**Reason/remediation:** Uncorrected acknowledgment corrupts the shared picture. Revisit Q102-05 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-05-L2

No acknowledgment after sending a report: is it complete?

**Answer:** No. Use the approved fallback and confirm receipt.

**Reason/remediation:** Sent is not received. Revisit Q102-05 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-06-L1

An assistance helper is absent. Mark assistance complete?

**Answer:** No. Request the approved replacement and report the need.

**Reason/remediation:** Planned resources may not be available. Revisit Q102-06 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-06-L2

Does the initial count stay current when people leave?

**Answer:** No. Record authorized movement and report updated status.

**Reason/remediation:** Presence is time-dependent. Revisit Q102-06 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-07-L1

Eight depart, seven unique arrive plus a duplicate. What is confirmed?

**Answer:** Seven received and one identity unresolved in transit.

**Reason/remediation:** Eight entries do not establish eight arrivals. Revisit Q102-07 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-07-L2

People are queued for transport. Are they evacuated?

**Answer:** No. Record the actual stage and obtain departure/receipt evidence.

**Reason/remediation:** A plan is not completed movement. Revisit Q102-07 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-08-L1

Six paper arrivals reappear electronically. Count twelve?

**Answer:** No. Reconcile the same six identities across sources.

**Reason/remediation:** Duplicate data is not additional people. Revisit Q102-08 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-08-L2

What must relief accept?

**Answer:** Current roster, identities/locations, discrepancies, movements, hazards and unacknowledged reports.

**Reason/remediation:** Duty transfer requires a reliable picture. Revisit Q102-08 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-09-L1

Written pass but no workplace fallback demonstration: complete?

**Answer:** No. Obtain the missing observed evidence.

**Reason/remediation:** Knowledge does not replace workplace performance. Revisit Q102-09 and perform its changed application.

## Q102-09-L2

**Answer:** Remediate its cause and observe a changed uncoached drill.

**Reason/remediation:** Repeating a memorized sequence can conceal the gap. Revisit Q102-09 and perform its changed application.
