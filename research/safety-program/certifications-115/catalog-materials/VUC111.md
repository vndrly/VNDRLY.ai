# VUC111 — NDT Level II Method Practice

Original VNDRLY/U common core and PT/MT/UT/RT/VT teaching tracks. External ASNT9712 and employer/regulatory authorization are separate.

## Q111-01 — Certification scope and Level II responsibility

Nondestructive testing evaluates selected characteristics without intentionally destroying the item. A Level II role is method- and technique-specific: competence in penetrant testing does not establish ultrasonic or radiographic competence. Define method, technique, product/sector, procedure and limitations on every assignment and credential. Distinguish an independent certification scheme from an employer's written practice and job authorization. ASNT 9712 and ASNT NDT Level II are different programs; their names must not be used interchangeably.

This original VNDRLY package includes common instruction and PT, MT, UT, RT and VT learning tracks. It does not issue ASNT 9712, grant regulatory radiation authority or create required industrial experience. Each learner's demonstrated method/technique is recorded separately. Public ASNT pages currently differ on VT availability: the program page labels it coming soon while another overview includes it. Verify availability directly before an external application; the original VT instruction remains useful without claiming that enrollment is open.

Before testing, confirm identity, current procedure, acceptance criteria, equipment capability, training/experience, vision requirements and employer authorization. A Level II may prepare an instruction within the authorized procedure, but cannot invent a new acceptance rule to clear a part. Stop when scope, safety or technique capability is uncertain. Worked decision: an MT-trained worker is asked to approve an aluminum casting by MT. Reject the method/material mismatch and refer selection of an appropriate authorized method.

## Q111-02 — Materials, manufacturing and discontinuities

Understand how manufacturing and service create the features a method may reveal. Castings can contain shrinkage, porosity and inclusions; forgings can contain laps or internal discontinuities; welds can contain lack of fusion, incomplete penetration, porosity, slag and cracking. Service can introduce fatigue, corrosion, erosion or stress-related damage. Geometry such as roots, edges, threads and material transitions can also produce indications.

An indication is a response requiring interpretation. A relevant indication arises from a condition of interest; a nonrelevant indication may arise from geometry or known structure; a false indication may arise from processing or equipment artifacts. A discontinuity is not automatically a rejectable defect. Rejection requires the applicable acceptance criteria and valid examination. Do not dismiss an unexpected response as geometry without supporting evidence.

Orientation and accessibility govern detectability. PT needs an opening to the surface; MT needs ferromagnetic material and a suitable field direction; UT depends on sound path, orientation, coupling and material response; RT depends on attenuation, geometry and projection; VT depends on access, lighting and resolution. Worked comparison: a tight planar flaw parallel to the radiation beam may be difficult to reveal by RT, while an appropriately oriented UT beam may respond strongly. Method selection must address the expected flaw, not merely the equipment on hand.

## Q111-03 — Instructions, calibration and examination control

An executable instruction identifies the item and area, governing procedure/revision, personnel requirements, safety, equipment/consumables, preparation, settings, calibration/system checks, coverage, interpretation, acceptance, reporting and post-test actions. It must be clear enough for an authorized operator to reproduce. Copying a generic checklist without item geometry or criteria is insufficient.

Distinguish instrument calibration from a pre-use system performance check and technique calibration on representative reference standards. Record equipment IDs and validity, reference-block or comparator identity, settings, environmental conditions and checks before/after examination. If a post-check fails, determine the affected interval and reinspect under the approved process; do not assume earlier results remain valid.

Original control example: examinations E1–E6 occur between a passing 08:00 check and failing 10:00 check, with no intermediate check. Their validity is unresolved until the approved investigation defines the affected interval; simply repeating E6 is not justified. Preserve original reports and corrections. Acceptance criteria supplied in this course are fictional training cards, not ASTM/ASME/AWS/ISO provisions. Real work must use the current applicable procedure and qualified approval. Safety includes chemicals, UV exposure, electrical equipment, access, confined spaces and radiation as relevant.

## Q111-04 — Liquid penetrant testing

PT uses a penetrant capable of entering surface-breaking discontinuities on suitable nonporous materials. Clean and dry the surface using the approved method without smearing metal over openings. Verify penetrant family, sensitivity/type, removal process, compatible developer, batch/expiry and temperature range. Apply penetrant uniformly, allow the specified dwell, remove excess by the correct process, dry as required, apply developer and inspect within the prescribed development interval under verified viewing conditions.

Solvent-removable, water-washable and post-emulsifiable systems have different removal controls. Excessive washing or solvent can remove penetrant from discontinuities; inadequate removal produces background that masks indications. Emulsification time and method are critical for post-emulsifiable systems. Do not mix materials from unapproved systems or improvise dwell/temperature values. Fluorescent and visible methods require their respective viewing controls and measured light conditions; record the actual procedure limits.

Interpret indication shape, size, location and behavior, distinguishing relevant bleed-out from surface contamination or process marks. Measure according to the applicable instruction, not a guessed flaw size beneath the indication. Worked training card: reject any relevant linear indication longer than 3.0 mm; a 4.0 mm linear bleed-out rejects, a 2.0 mm rounded indication requires the separate rounded criterion, and heavy background invalidates examination. Equality and classification rules must be defined. Clean/protect the part after inspection and retain traceable disposition. PT does not detect sealed subsurface flaws.

## Q111-05 — Magnetic particle testing

MT detects surface and near-surface discontinuities in ferromagnetic materials by observing particles accumulated at magnetic flux leakage. Confirm material suitability. Choose an authorized magnetizing technique, particle medium and viewing conditions. A field approximately perpendicular to the expected discontinuity generally improves detection; examining in two appropriate directions helps address orientation limitations. A single direction cannot justify universal coverage.

Yokes and bench systems create different field arrangements. Verify equipment performance with the prescribed checks, contact/spacing where applicable, field adequacy/direction and particle system condition. Current magnitude alone does not prove an effective field at the examination area. Avoid arc burns, overheating and unsafe electrical contact; direct-contact techniques require their specific controls. Apply particles and magnetization in the specified sequence and observe under controlled lighting.

Geometry, permeability changes and residual fields can create nonrelevant indications. Investigate without grinding away evidence or changing acceptance rules. Demagnetize when required and verify the residual field using the prescribed method; post-clean and protect the part. Worked case: a weld is tested with one yoke orientation and no field verification. A blank surface is not a valid no-defect result. Repeat under the approved technique with field/coverage checks. The course's safe yoke demonstration is not qualification for every bench/prod/coil technique.

## Q111-06 — Ultrasonic principles and straight-beam testing

UT introduces sound and interprets returned or transmitted signals. In pulse-echo thickness measurement, distance equals velocity times round-trip time divided by two after correct zero/delay compensation. Material, temperature, mode and geometry affect velocity. Couplant transfers sound across the interface; poor coupling can mimic loss of backwall. Frequency and probe size affect resolution, penetration and beam behavior. Near-field and beam-spread effects matter when comparing amplitudes.

For a supplied longitudinal velocity 5900 m/s and corrected round-trip time 20 microseconds, thickness is 59.0 mm. If time is not corrected for probe delay, the result can be biased. Calibrate range, zero and sensitivity with appropriate reference blocks and verify at relevant ranges. Use representative material/curvature where required. Record probe, frequency, instrument, calibration, couplant and temperature.

A reduced backwall echo may result from attenuation, roughness, coupling, geometry or a discontinuity. Check technique and compare with known features before assigning a cause. Scan with specified overlap and coverage, recording inaccessible zones. For castings/forgings, grain structure and geometry can affect scattering and detectability. Worked decision: calibration on a flat steel block is transferred to a curved coarse-grained component without procedure support. Hold the examination until technique applicability and sensitivity are established; a numeric thickness display alone is not valid evidence.

## Q111-07 — Ultrasonic angle-beam and weld evaluation

Angle-beam examination directs a refracted beam through the material to interrogate weld regions. Confirm wedge/probe identity, refracted angle, index point, range, sensitivity and applicable reference response. Snell's relation connects incident and refracted modes through their velocities, but real wedges and materials require verification rather than a nameplate assumption. Account for weld geometry, thickness, access and expected flaw orientation.

For a supplied 60-degree refracted angle in a 20 mm plate, first-leg surface distance is 20 tan 60=34.64 mm and sound path 20/cos 60=40.0 mm. A full skip surface distance is 69.28 mm. These calculations describe ideal geometry, not a flaw's exact dimensions. Locate indications with the verified index and sound path, then evaluate by the governing procedure. Mode conversion, roots and opposite-surface reflections can produce responses requiring interpretation.

DAC or other reference methods compare responses under controlled calibration; transfer corrections and material attenuation must follow the approved method. Amplitude is not automatically flaw height. Sizing techniques have their own limitations. Worked case: a response exceeds a fictional evaluation level but lies at the expected root path. Record and investigate using permitted scans/angles; do not automatically accept as root geometry or reject without applying the criterion. Advanced phased-array or TOFD techniques require separate specific training and procedures; conventional UT instruction does not silently qualify them.

## Q111-08 — Radiographic principles, geometry and radiation boundary

RT forms an image from differential transmission of ionizing radiation through an object. Thickness, density, composition, beam energy, scatter and geometry influence image contrast. X-ray generators and gamma sources differ in energy/control and regulatory handling. Film, computed radiography and digital detector systems require their own acquisition and quality controls; skill with one does not automatically qualify another.

Geometric unsharpness can be modeled as Ug=F×b/a, where F is effective source size, b object-to-detector distance and a source-to-object distance in a consistent geometry. In an original example F2 mm, b 20 mm, a 400 mm givesUg 0.10 mm. Increasing a can improve geometry but changes exposure requirements. A simple inverse-square model says doubling distance reduces unattenuated intensity to one quarter; restoring receptor exposure under otherwise identical conditions requires four times exposure. Scatter, attenuation, energy and detector behavior limit simplistic calculations.

All RT exercises in this written package use inert layouts and synthetic image-quality records. Live gamma/X-ray work requires the licensed/authorized organization, trained personnel, radiation-safety program, barriers, surveys, monitoring and emergency procedures applicable to the jurisdiction. Never approach a suspected exposed or stuck source, defeat interlocks or attempt recovery. Withdraw/control access and notify the radiation-safety authority under the approved emergency plan. An NDT training pass is not radiation authorization.

## Q111-09 — Radiographic quality, processing and interpretation

Before evaluating a radiograph, verify item/area identification, orientation, coverage, technique, image-quality indicator arrangement and required image quality. An IQI demonstrates specified image-quality capability under the technique; it is not a physical replica of every possible flaw. Film processing, handling and viewing can introduce artifacts; digital systems require validated acquisition, calibration, resolution and display controls. Do not treat arbitrary image enhancement as equivalent to the original data.

Check the applicable density/contrast/resolution requirements and viewing conditions. Retain original images and processing metadata. A scratch, water mark, detector defect or stitching artifact may resemble a discontinuity. Compare location, morphology, repeated views and process evidence, but do not erase an indication merely because it is inconvenient. Planar defects may be missed when orientation is unfavorable; negative RT is bounded by technique sensitivity and coverage.

Training image card R111 supplies fictional acceptance rules and measured features. ImageA has valid quality and a 5 mm linear relevant indication; ImageB has insufficient quality; ImageC has a known processing scratch outside the part plus a 2 mm rounded relevant indication. EvaluateA against the linear criterion, rejectB as an examination before judging the part, and keepC's artifact separate from its relevant feature. A schematic is useful for reasoning but cannot replace actual image interpretation practice in a fully equipped authorized method course. Learner endorsements must reflect the actual practical evidence obtained.

## Q111-10 — Visual testing and remote observation

VT is an examination method requiring defined access, lighting, resolution, surface condition and viewing geometry. Direct viewing and remote optics have different limitations. Establish item identity and coverage, clean only as permitted, verify measuring tools and document dimensions with scale and orientation. A photograph without scale or location can be visually persuasive but technically unusable.

Remote cameras/borescopes introduce distortion, perspective, depth-of-field, illumination and blind-zone issues. Verify system performance using suitable targets and the actual working configuration. A nominal pixel count does not guarantee defect resolution at the inspection distance. Record distance/angle or other procedure-required geometry, lens settings and coverage gaps. Do not infer unseen surface condition from adjacent clear images.

Worked example: a supplied image scale is 0.20 mm/pixel in the calibrated plane; a feature spans 15 pixels, giving 3.0 mm in that plane. If the feature lies out of plane or the lens distance changes, the scale may no longer apply. Repeat with valid geometry before acceptance. Distinguish discoloration, surface contamination, dimensional mismatch and suspected cracking; use the governing criterion and refer another method when needed. Access to an enclosed item may introduce confined-space or isolation hazards not removed by choosing a camera.

## Q111-11 — Evaluation, instruction preparation and reporting

Interpretation asks what caused the indication; evaluation asks whether the identified condition meets the applicable criteria. Keep those steps separate. State the procedure and acceptance source/revision, measured indication characteristics, validity checks and disposition. A Level II instruction should specify how an authorized operator performs the defined examination, including coverage, calibration, process parameters, recording and escalation. It must not exceed the governing procedure's authority.

Original acceptance card A111: relevant linear indications longer than 3.0 mm reject; relevant rounded indications greater than 2.5 mm reject; three or more rounded indications in any 10 mm evaluation length reject; exactly 3.0 mm linear and 2.5 mm rounded meet these size limits only. A valid examination is required first. These rules are fictional and not a code excerpt. For three rounded indications of 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0 mm within 8 mm, size alone passes but grouping rejects. Record both reasoning steps.

Report coverage and limitations even when no relevant indication is found. Identify repairs/re-examinations with traceable links, retaining original results. Do not state an entire component is defect-free when only one accessible area was tested. Worked instruction defect: it says 'inspect weld and accept if good' with no revision, coverage or criteria. Return it for completion; an experienced operator's intuition cannot make it reproducible.

## Q111-12 — Quality, ethics, practical evidence and continuing competence

Quality depends on traceable personnel, equipment, procedures, consumables, specimens and records. Protect controlled examination materials and avoid coaching during scored practical work. Record actual method/technique exposure and distinguish training hours from industrial experience. Vision and continuing-activity requirements belong to the applicable scheme and employer system; do not invent compliance from classroom attendance.

When equipment or process checks fail, identify affected examinations, preserve evidence, notify the responsible authority and perform the approved retest. Pressure to meet a shipment deadline does not justify changing criteria, omitting an indication or signing another person's work. A qualified reviewer should resolve disputed interpretation using the procedure and evidence, not rank or commercial pressure.

This package provides instruction, synthetic datasets, specimen-preparation specifications, observation forms, final and changed assessments. Actual learner endorsement requires the specified supervised practical demonstrations for that method/technique; RT exposure remains within an authorized radiation program. Missing equipment means pending practical evidence, not automatic completion. Issue an original training record with exact scope and limitations, never an ASNT9712 certificate. Renewal/external eligibility and method availability must be confirmed with the current certification body. Authoring completion is separate from an individual's qualification.

# VUC111 method workbooks, instruction preparation and assessment

## Delivery routes and resources

Common core 16 hours. Original method allocations: PT32, MT40, UT80, RT80, VT24; all-track plan 272 hours. These allocations are a teaching plan, not ASNT minimum-hour equivalence. Within each track allocate approximately one quarter instruction/worked examples, one half supervised practical and one quarter interpretation/instruction preparation/assessment; extend for remediation. Record actual attendance and practice by method/technique. Core may be reused, but no method endorsement is inferred from another. Diagnose arithmetic, units, drawing interpretation and safe-work understanding first. Verify applicable vision requirements through the authorized scheme/employer process.

Instructors/assessors must be qualified and experienced in the method/technique and the governing delivery procedure. A LevelIII or other properly authorized procedure authority controls actual technique instructions and acceptance references. Provide accessible written diagrams and calibrated viewing arrangements; accommodations preserve detection and independent evaluation requirements.

PT resources: approved compatible consumable system, known surface-opening reference panels, clean nonporous coupons, timer, temperature measurement, applicable visible/UV viewing meters and safety data. MT: safe approved yoke and bench training arrangements where that technique is endorsed, ferromagnetic known-discontinuity specimens, field/system indicators, particles, light meters and residual-field equipment. UT: instrument, straight/angle probes, wedges, couplant, suitable known reference blocks and mapped casting/forging/weld specimens. RT: inert geometry rig, actual authorized training image sets with known truth, viewers/display quality tools and, only at a licensed facility, approved exposure equipment/safety program. VT: known dimensional/surface specimens, calibrated gauges, illumination meter, direct/remote optics and resolution targets. Missing resources defer the method's practical endorsement.

Use A111 fictional acceptance card only for synthetic exercises: linear relevant indication>3.0 mm rejects; rounded>2.5 mm rejects; at least 3 rounded in any 10 mm evaluation length rejects. A valid exam is prerequisite. These are not code values. Actual specimens are graded against the delivery procedure's approved criteria and assessor master map, not invented field rules.

## Instructor specimen preparation and control

Prepare three specimens per endorsed technique: one sound with geometric distractor, one relevant planar/surface indication and one volumetric/grouped or mixed indication appropriate to the method. A qualified assessor establishes the master map independently using suitable validated methods, recording identity, coordinates, dimensions, orientations, detectable technique and uncertainty. Mark learner-facing IDs without revealing flaw locations. Do not damage production parts to create specimens. Use purchased/verified training blocks or nonservice coupons. Keep master maps private. Retire specimens whose condition changes; pre/post-check them for contamination, wear or damage.

Synthetic card set S111 provides reproducible reasoning exercises: S1 valid 4.0 mm linear indication at x 20, y 10; S2 rounded 1.0/1.5/2.0 mm at x 10/13/18, y 20; S3 valid 2.0 mm rounded at x 40, y 15 plus known artifact outside part; S4 inadequate system/image quality; S5 no relevant indications but 20% area inaccessible; S6 linear 3.0 mm and rounded 2.5 mm with no group. Coordinates in mm on a 100×50 mm coupon. These are interpretation cards, not evidence of detection skill. Learners must separately locate unknown indications on actual approved training specimens for practical endorsement.
