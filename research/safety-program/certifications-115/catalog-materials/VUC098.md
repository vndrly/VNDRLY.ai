# VNDRLY/U NUI HLO / Helicopter Emergency Response Team Leader — Workplace Competence

VUC098. Original available-source combined-role assessment materials. Actual workplace evidence and local emergency capability govern the outcome. No external accreditation, aircraft authority or specialist refuelling/rescue qualification is conferred.

## Q98-1 — Defining the combined workplace role

Define precisely which HLO and emergency-team-leader duties the employer assigns on the NUI or not-permanently-attended installation. The title does not automatically grant every aviation, rescue or maintenance function. Establish the normal operating cycle, the emergency command interface and the limits of the team’s actual capability. Separate specialist helicopter refuelling training and assessment remain necessary where that work is assigned.

Review initial preparation, supervised workplace development, fitness and relevant specialist evidence before practical assessment. The public external scheme has its own entry wording; this original package still requires safe readiness. The assessor must be competent in the actual role and local procedures, and must not assess tasks that neither the candidate nor the assessor is authorized to perform. An award is a reasoned evidence decision, not an assumption based on job title.

Worked and changed applications: A candidate is competent in normal HLO routines but has no evidence for the assigned emergency-leadership duties. Do not label the combined role complete. Changed case: refuelling is added to the job description. Require separate specialist preparation and evidence rather than treating the combined title as coverage.

## Q98-2 — Assessment plan and authentic workplace evidence

Map each duty to direct observation, authenticated work products, questioning and safe scenario evidence. Record the site, operator, equipment, procedure revision and candidate contribution. Two independent routine workplace cycles are required by this original package, alongside local emergency exercises and changed-condition assessment. A generic witness signature without task, date or independent action is insufficient.

Rare emergencies should be assessed through approved safe drills or simulation, not by creating real failures. Label simulation honestly and identify its limits. Normal workplace evidence must come from actual authorized operations; a desktop exercise cannot be relabeled as a real flight cycle. Preserve privacy using secure references or redacted records. A reviewer checks sufficiency, consistency and any conflict of interest before the outcome.

Worked and changed applications: A portfolio contains team photographs and old checklists but no attribution. Authenticate specific actions and observe the candidate independently. Changed case: one cycle was heavily prompted. Record it as development and obtain a changed independent observation rather than hiding the assistance.

## Q98-3 — Local operating concept and transition ownership

The NUI operating concept must cover first arrival, attended work and final departure, with clear remote, crew and visiting-team responsibilities. Verify current local procedures and restrictions. An assessment should require the candidate to trace who supplies status, who accepts handover and what happens when information or capability is missing. Do not invent a resident team during unattended phases.

Check that the plan remains achievable with actual staffing, communications, installed protection and external support. If the visiting team is expected to inspect before it can arrive, identify the impossible sequence and seek authorized resolution. If the final remote recipient is unavailable, the handover requirement remains unresolved. Competence includes recognizing a broken plan and preserving a hold, not merely completing its form.

Worked and changed applications: Remote support assumes the HLO is already on site during the first landing. Correct the responsibility gap through the approved operating concept. Changed case: the return flight is delayed into a different support shift. Confirm continuity and current status before final handover.

## Q98-4 — Readiness information and equipment conditions

Use current approved information on deck condition, aircraft interfaces, weather, motion where applicable, equipment and restrictions. Separate measurement time from display-refresh time and confirm location, units and fault status. A stale or wrong-site report is not repaired by a confident voice. The HLO supplies accurate local information; aircraft acceptance remains with the responsible operator and crew.

Apply unserviceability provisions only under their actual conditions, authority and notifications. A listed alternative must be established and remain available. If it fails, update restrictions immediately. Do not bypass a safety indication or invent a favorable reading to keep a flight moving. The assessor should trace source data and the candidate’s reasoning, including appropriate uncertainty and escalation.

Worked and changed applications: A display updates at 08:10 but the sample is from 07:45; at 08:15 the sample is thirty minutes old. Changed case: an approved relay alternative is established, then drops out. The earlier approval no longer proves required communication capability.

## Q98-5 — Deck inspection and concurrent-work coordination

Observe systematic inspection when access is safe and authorized: loose objects, contamination, routes, required markings/equipment and current defects. Findings must have an owner, restriction and verified resolution. A ticked checklist without actual observation is not evidence. Never introduce a real foreign object or obstruction during live operations for assessment.

Coordinate cranes, work parties and other conflicting activities through approved controls. Distinguish receipt of a request from confirmation of the required actual state. A corrected crane condition does not resolve a separate blocked passenger route. The candidate must keep the current status coherent and communicate new hazards after readiness has been reported. Use safe mock injects for conditions that cannot be introduced operationally.

Worked and changed applications: Crane contact says message received, while movement continues. Obtain verified safe status before dependent readiness. Changed case: crane is secured but a route remains contaminated; preserve that separate hold and recheck correction.

## Q98-6 — Team leadership and routine movement control

Brief team roles, hazards, approved routes, communications, special passenger arrangements and stop criteria. Confirm understanding and current required staffing. Keep access controlled during arrival, disembarkation, loading, boarding and departure through the actual crew/HLO interface. Generic training diagrams do not establish a universal safe aircraft approach direction.

A competent leader notices when the plan is no longer shared. Ambiguous radio messages, changed routes or a team member who cannot repeat the task require correction. Do not proceed based on seniority or pressure. Observe how the candidate responds to a safety challenge and how instructions reach every affected person. The goal is safe coherent movement, not the shortest turnaround.

Worked and changed applications: The team is briefed on R1 but the approved route changes to R2. Rebrief and confirm before movement. Changed case: a passenger drops an object near a hazard area. Stop pursuit and arrange safe recovery rather than allowing an uncontrolled approach.

## Q98-7 — Passenger, item and dangerous-goods reconciliation

Confirm actual identities and current manifest, not just matching headcount. Reconcile removed or substituted passengers with their bags and destination. Late freight changes require verified data and authorized acceptance. The HLO coordinates the interface but does not independently approve aircraft mass/balance or accept dangerous goods beyond assigned competence.

Keep actual items controlled after weighing and match destination and sector. Recognize suspect items, preserve safe holds and use the authorized specialist route. Do not hide freight as baggage or guess battery capacity. Assess a changed identity/item case and require the candidate to explain how current information reaches crew and ground records. Arithmetic accuracy alone cannot resolve a wrong person or unaccepted item.

Worked and changed applications: Five people are expected and five present, but one ID differs. Hold and resolve identity/eligibility and current manifest. Changed case: a toolbox is added after weighing. Verify the changed input, revise records and obtain required acceptance; the earlier total is obsolete.

## Q98-8 — Emergency preplan and actual team capability

Review the local emergency plan against available people, installed protection, communications, escape routes and external support. Identify tasks requiring specialist competence and actions the visiting team can perform safely. A combined role title does not create breathing-apparatus, rescue or medical capability. Avoid assigning one person incompatible simultaneous duties.

Prepare for loss of a team member, blocked access, failed protection and delayed assistance. Establish stop/withdrawal criteria and the command interface. The assessor must see a realistic plan and a response to changed resources, not a copied generic document. Drills should test judgment and coordination without exposing participants to fire, smoke, rotors or hazardous entry.

Worked and changed applications: The plan assumes three trained responders but only two are available for the visit. Escalate and obtain an achievable authorized arrangement. Changed case: adequate staffing remains but one required protection resource fails; people do not automatically substitute for engineered capability.

## Q98-9 — Emergency leadership and communication

At emergency onset, raise the alarm through the approved route, state location and confirmed facts, protect people and coordinate the assigned response. Keep unknowns explicit. Allocate tasks with clear owners, obtain confirmation and monitor results. Maintain communication with crew, remote support and the responsible incident authority without claiming command beyond the actual role.

Reassess priorities as conditions change. A safe route can become unavailable, a casualty report can be corrected and an alternative link can fail. Update affected personnel and confirm understanding. Challenge instructions that depend on unverified conditions or exceed capability. The assessor records the candidate’s decisions and timing context, but does not reward unsafe haste or unsupported certainty.

Worked and changed applications: A roleplayer demands a precise casualty count before reconciliation. Report confirmed people and unknowns, then update from evidence. Changed case: the instruction names the wrong muster point. Correct and confirm the current safe destination before movement.

## Q98-10 — Protection controls, accountability and safe support

Manage installed-system indications and authorized controls through the actual procedure. Activation, cessation or reset does not automatically prove hazard control. Verify prerequisites before any authorized action, and do not bypass or isolate protection merely to restore a ready display. Simulation uses an inert panel and documented decision conditions, not real safety-system manipulation.

Account for unique identities and verified locations, retaining missing-person information and updates. A duplicate entry cannot offset an absent person. Use approved safe response and assistance within actual training; do not send unprotected personnel to search. Give authorized responders accurate casualty and location information. The combined role requires leadership of available capability, not invention of unavailable capability.

Worked and changed applications: Expected seven people; seven tally entries include a duplicate and omit one identity. Report six unique accounted for and the missing person. Changed case: a system action is authorized only after condition C, but C is unknown. Preserve the hold while seeking verification.

## Q98-11 — Recovery, final departure and unattended handover

After normal or abnormal operations, verify actual current readiness, equipment restoration, people/items and remaining restrictions. Final departure removes local personnel, so the required protection state and remote ownership must be established through the approved plan. Do not leave an unresolved isolation or unknown status concealed behind a completed checklist.

Provide a current handover with event summary, verified state, pending defects, owners and next actions. Confirm the correct recipient understands the current revision. Preserve source records and corrections for review. If required unattended state cannot be established, escalate the contingency rather than falsely signing complete. The assessor should trace the handover to actual evidence and recipient confirmation.

Worked and changed applications: All people are accounted for, but a required resource remains unavailable. Keep that restriction visible and obtain authorized disposition before the relevant readiness claim. Changed case: recipient acknowledges the wrong revision. Correct and reconfirm; transmission alone is not accurate handover.

## Q98-12 — Competence decision, remediation and scope maintenance

Combine independent normal-work observations, local emergency drills, changed scenarios, authenticated records and knowledge evidence. Every mandatory domain must be supported. A high final score or a large portfolio does not offset a critical practical failure. Record prompts and distinguish development from independent performance. A reviewer checks the scope and evidence before an original outcome is issued.

Remediate the specific control and reassess a changed task without erasing the first failure. Record operator/site/system context, limitations and required review triggers. This original VNDRLY package does not issue OPITO certification, renew an external credential or automatically appoint the learner. Changes in duties, equipment or operating concept require review and additional evidence where needed.

Worked and changed applications: Candidate performs normal cycles independently but emergency decisions were coached. Withhold the combined outcome until changed independent emergency evidence passes. Changed case: the candidate correctly refuses an unsupported reset under pressure. Credit the control and assess remaining requirements rather than penalizing delay.

# VUC098 workplace assessor and exercise workbook

## Assessment environment and safe evidence

Use an assessor competent in the local combined HLO/HERTL role and evidence assessment, with an independent competent reviewer. Verify initial preparation, supervised development, fitness and current specialist prerequisites appropriate to duties. Public external entry wording does not remove the duty to assess safely. No learner is placed in an unprepared live role to obtain evidence.

F98 context: employer/site/operator___ normal duties___ emergency duties/command interface___ manual and emergency-plan revisions___ aircraft/system scope___ required team/protection___ first-arrival and final-departure concept___ approved communications/alternatives___ passenger/manifest interface___ specialist exclusions___ candidate preparation/fitness___ assessor/reviewer___ privacy/retention___. Actual approved procedures define operating criteria. Missing criteria are not replaced with classroom inventions.

Require two independently observed authorized routine workplace cycles W1/W2, covering preparation, arrival, people/items, departure and restoration. Record actual date, context, actions and secure source references. Require two approved local emergency drills D1/D2 that test the candidate's assigned leadership duties in the local operating concept; they may be non-hazardous simulations and must be labeled as such. The two supplied changed scenarios below provide drill scripts after local adaptation. Actual routine cycles cannot be replaced by simulation and still be called workplace competence.

Do not plant live defects, disable operational systems, alter real manifests, transmit false emergency traffic or create dangerous conditions. Observe real routine work without distracting crew. Drills use ground-level mock/inactive areas, inert aircraft and system panels, harmless props, paper casualty/DG cards, isolated radios and roleplayers. No live fire, fuel, smoke, rotor movement, elevated-edge exposure, hazardous entry or untrained casualty lifting. Independent safety controller checks readiness and stops any real risk. If local access is unavailable, record preparation/simulation only until the workplace evidence is complete.

## I98 integrated decision and rubric

Complete W1/W2 and both adapted D1/D2 scenarios with authenticated evidence. Controller releases facts sequentially, never coaches assessed decisions, and safety controller may stop immediately. Reviewer maps every domain to evidence and confirms both normal and emergency components. A simulation-only result remains explicitly limited.

Twelve dimensions match the stations. Score 0 absent/unsafe, 1 major coaching, 2 safe incomplete/prompted, 3 independently acceptable, 4 accurate independent performance with explanation. Require at least 39/48 for each integrated assessed cycle, every dimension at least 3, every mandatory task passed and no critical error. Critical: false readiness, unsafe access, ignored missing identity, invented status/location, unauthorized reset/isolation, unsupported rescue/DG/refuelling authority or fabricated evidence. Preserve failure, remediate and assess changed independent performance. A correct hold is credited even if it delays the exercise.
