# VNDRLY/U online learning experience

Research checked November 19, 2026. These are product recommendations, not new completion conditions or implemented features.

## What is already authorized

Self-paced online instruction in approximately 5–10 minute sections; explanations, reasoning and examples; suitable retention checks; practice only where useful; saved responses and a reliable return point. Employer-observed tasks may be appropriate for some skills, but are not automatically required. Shared courses retain one identity and one progress record across certifications. Course content is completed before benchmark and request UI is treated as finished.

## Comparisons and recommendations

**A course home that answers “What will I learn?” and “Where do I continue?”** Open University describes its module website as a home for materials, activities, a study planner and assessment information. For VNDRLY/U, put the course outcome, section list, estimated effort and saved return point together. Keep the existing View Course → actual course → Begin/Continue flow. A separate screen containing only a start button adds no instructional value. [Open University module tour](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ELPuTkOqyb8)

**Instruction, practice and assessment that serve the same outcome.** Carnegie Mellon recommends alignment between learning objectives, learning activities and assessment. VNDRLY/U should connect each retention check to the explanation that teaches its answer, and give feedback that explains the reasoning. Do not add a scenario merely to satisfy a template; use it when applying a decision is the outcome. [Carnegie Mellon teaching principles](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/principles/teaching.html)

**Useful feedback and a visible learning record.** Carnegie Mellon identifies goal-directed practice and targeted feedback as learning principles. Preserve the learner’s original answer, later corrections and the explanation reviewed. Show those as learning history, not as an assertion that a corrected answer was correct on the first attempt. [Carnegie Mellon learning principles](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/principles/learning.html)

**A personal study view.** OpenLearn offers enrollment and progress tracking for its courses. VNDRLY/U could give each user a compact “Continue learning” list of assigned courses with the next section and remaining estimated effort. Avoid mandatory schedules or countdowns: the user requested stopping and returning at any time. [OpenLearn course guidance](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/get-started/frequently-asked-questions/how-start-course)

**Optional reference access during learning.** OpenLearn's online-teaching course explains navigation and includes activities in its course pages. For VNDRLY/U, keep a course outline and reference drawer available without losing the current response. Reference pages, source registers and instructor notes should not become separate courses. [OpenLearn navigation and activities](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/mod/oucontent/view.php?id=138072&section=2)

## Further improvements to consider, not silently implement

- Personal notes and bookmarks tied to a specific section.
- Optional narration with a readable transcript and user-controlled playback; no auto-playing audio or mandatory listening timer.
- Small original diagrams or demonstrations when text alone makes a procedure difficult to understand. Do not insert decorative media into every lesson.
- A “Report a problem with this lesson” action carrying the course and section reference.
- A practice review that revisits concepts the learner missed without erasing their earlier work or adding an unapproved prerequisite.
- Clear separation between online knowledge evidence, employer-observed task evidence and externally issued licenses.

Discussion forums, mandatory peer reviews, deadlines, required attendance and instructor availability are not proposed as prerequisites. The approved product is self-paced online learning, not a facility-led course.
