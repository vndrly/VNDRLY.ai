# Policy rewrite research record

Checked November 1, 2026. This file is an internal authoring record and is not appended to the policies.

The 59 policy documents replace the former adoption tables, common approval workflow, scoring commentary, research appendices and cross-resource link blocks. The documents state the subject's work practices and responsibilities. No document creates a universal course assessor, accreditation, sign-off, audit frequency or evidence submission condition. Training assignment settings remain company controlled.

Specific federal duties retained in the text are scoped to the operation governed by the cited rule:

| Policy | Legal basis and scope |
|---|---|

References to transport, specialist operations, substance-specific exposure duties and state rules are conditional on the actual service and jurisdiction; they do not create blanket credentials or administrative gates for all vendors.
