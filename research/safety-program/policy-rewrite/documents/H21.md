# Lone work and personal security

Lone and remote work is arranged so a worker can obtain help without taking avoidable risks. This policy applies to isolated assignments, remote support and gate or security work.

The supervisor and worker consider the location, communication coverage, travel and likely hazards. They agree how to contact one another and how help will be summoned if contact is lost. The contact method and timing suit the work; Work needing a second person under an applicable safety rule is not assigned as lone work.

Workers keep a means of communication available and let the supervisor know when the work location or return arrangement changes. An aggressive person is handled by creating distance, using a safe exit and calling for assistance. Workers are not expected to physically confront an intruder or recover property at the expense of personal safety.

If contact fails, the responsible person uses the agreed response, including emergency services when the circumstances indicate danger. A worker who cannot obtain essential help pauses the exposed activity until communication or support is restored.
