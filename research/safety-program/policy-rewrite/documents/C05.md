# Commercial driving roles

Commercial driving assignments match the driver, vehicle and service to the transport rules that govern them. This policy applies to regulated commercial motor vehicle operations.

The person assigning a vehicle confirms the class of driving involved and uses a driver with the appropriate license and legally required endorsements. The driver follows applicable qualification, medical, hours-of-service and drug-and-alcohol program requirements where the operation triggers them. Those requirements are not imposed on employees who do not perform the regulated driving role.

Before travel, the driver considers vehicle condition, load, route, weather and available driving time. Loads are distributed and secured for the vehicle and cargo. Defects affecting safe operation are corrected before travel. Drivers maintain the inspection and duty records required for their operation.

The driver stops safely when fatigued, impaired or faced with an unsafe vehicle. The supervisor arranges a safe alternative rather than requiring the trip to continue. A change in vehicle class, cargo or service is checked for different legal requirements before assignment.
