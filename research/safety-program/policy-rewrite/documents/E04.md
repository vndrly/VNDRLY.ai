# Gas detection equipment

Gas measurements use equipment suitable for the atmosphere and the decision being made. This policy applies to personal detectors and instruments used for atmospheric testing.

The user confirms the gases measured, sensor range, alarm settings and limitations of the instrument. Readiness checks, functional testing and calibration follow the manufacturer's instructions and the requirements of the task. An instrument that cannot respond correctly, has a failed sensor or is outside its required calibration condition is not used to declare an atmosphere safe.

Sampling considers the location of people, possible gas layering, ventilation and the time needed for a remote sample to reach the sensor. Oxygen, flammability and toxic readings are interpreted for the instrument and hazard involved. A single clear reading does not establish that conditions cannot change.

An alarm prompts the worker to follow the escape or entry procedure for the task, not to silence the alarm and continue. Suspect results are checked from a safe position with suitable equipment.
