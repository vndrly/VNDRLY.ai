# Traffic, gate access and pedestrian safety

Vehicle and pedestrian movements are arranged to avoid collisions and crushing. This policy applies to gates, yards, delivery areas and work around mobile equipment.

Drivers follow the site's routes and traffic directions, approach gates at a controlled speed and stop where directed. Gate and yard staff stand outside vehicle paths and avoid positions between a vehicle and a fixed object. Pedestrians use separated routes where available and do not assume an operator can see them.

Before reversing or moving into a congested area, the driver confirms the route is clear. When a spotter is used, the driver and spotter agree the signals and stop if communication is lost. Barriers and warning devices are positioned so they guide traffic without creating a new obstruction or trapping a person.

Gate staff do not enter a moving lane to resolve a dispute or inspect a vehicle. An access problem is handled from a safe position. A collision, near miss or change that removes pedestrian separation is reported to the supervisor, and the affected movement is held while a safe arrangement is restored.
