# Pressure equipment and hose integrity

Pressure equipment is selected, connected and maintained to contain its intended service. This policy applies to hoses, pipework, vessels, pumps and relief devices.

The person arranging the system confirms the working pressure, temperature, fluid and compatibility of components. Connections are made with the correct fittings and restraints for the arrangement. Hoses are protected from traffic, sharp edges, excessive bending and other damage. Relief devices are suited to the protected system and are not blocked or adjusted to overcome an operating problem.

Users look for wear, corrosion, leakage, damaged couplings and abnormal movement. Equipment with a defect affecting pressure integrity is removed from the affected service. A pressurized connection is not tightened, opened or repaired. Pressure is safely released and the isolated condition checked before maintenance.

If a hose fails or pressure control is lost, workers withdraw from the release path and the operator stops the supply where it can be done safely. Inspection and certification requirements apply to the equipment for which the governing rules require them;
