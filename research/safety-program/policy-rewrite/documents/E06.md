# Lifting gear, access equipment and fall protection

Equipment supporting a person or load is suitable for its purpose and kept free of defects that could cause failure. This policy applies to rigging, ladders, scaffolds and personal fall-protection equipment.

Users check the condition and identification relevant to the equipment before use. Slings and connectors are compatible with the load and attachment. Ladders have sound rails, rungs and feet and are placed to remain stable. Harnesses, lanyards and anchors form a compatible system with adequate clearance for the intended work.

Damaged equipment, items with unreadable capacity information needed for safe use, and fall-protection components subjected to an impact are taken out of use and handled according to the applicable standard and manufacturer instructions. Workers do not improvise repairs to load-bearing or life-support components.

Scaffold inspections, sling inspections and personal fall-protection inspections follow the rules for the actual equipment and work. A missing guardrail, damaged sling or unsuitable anchor is resolved before the person or load is exposed.
