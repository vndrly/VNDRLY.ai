# Heat, cold and severe weather

Weather and temperature are considered when arranging outdoor work and physically demanding tasks. This policy applies to people exposed to heat, cold, lightning, wind or other severe weather.

The supervisor considers workload, clothing, humidity, sun, wind and each worker's adjustment to the conditions. Drinking water, suitable rest opportunities and access to cooling or warming are arranged for the exposure. Work can be moved, rescheduled or reduced when conditions make the planned method unsafe. New or returning workers receive attention while adjusting to heat or cold.

Workers watch for illness in themselves and coworkers. Confusion, collapse or suspected heat stroke is an emergency: call for medical help and begin appropriate cooling without delaying the call. Cold-related loss of coordination, numbness or altered behavior also requires prompt action.

During lightning, high winds or flooding, crews leave exposed positions and use suitable shelter. Work resumes when the specific hazard has passed and the area is safe. Applicable state heat rules and task-specific weather limits are followed where they govern;
