# Vehicles, trailers and loads

Vehicles and trailers are used within their capacity and with loads controlled for travel. This policy applies to company transport, deliveries and towing.

The driver checks that the vehicle, hitch, trailer and load are compatible. Brakes, tires, lights and safety connections are in working condition. Cargo is distributed without overloading axles or defeating steering and braking, and is secured against movement. Doors, ramps and loose items are secured before the vehicle moves.

Loading areas keep people clear of vehicle movement and falling material. Drivers consider clearance, grade and road conditions when choosing a route. The operator does not move a load that obscures essential visibility or exceeds equipment limits. Regulated commercial transport follows its applicable inspection and cargo-securement rules.

For a breakdown or stuck vehicle, people move clear of traffic and tensioned recovery equipment. Recovery uses suitable attachment points and rated equipment; a tow ball or improvised connection is not used as a recovery anchor. If safe recovery cannot be arranged, the driver obtains appropriate assistance rather than exposing coworkers to a failed connection.
