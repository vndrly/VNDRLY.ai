# Chemical information and exposure control

People receive the information and protection needed to use hazardous chemicals safely. This policy applies to chemicals brought into, stored or used in company work.

The company maintains the hazard-communication program required for its hazardous chemicals, including labels, accessible safety data sheets and employee information and training. Workers read the product label and relevant instructions before use and keep containers identified. Chemicals are stored so incompatible substances do not mix and containers are protected from damage.

The supervisor considers how the chemical is used, including mist, vapor, dust and skin contact. Substitution, containment and ventilation are used as appropriate before relying on personal protection alone. Workers use the correct protective equipment and keep food and drink away from contaminated areas.

A spill, unexpected reaction or exposure is reported promptly. Workers leave a dangerous area and obtain emergency help when needed. They do not mix chemicals or improvise cleanup with an incompatible product. Where exposure monitoring or medical surveillance is required by a substance-specific standard, it applies to the affected work and employees.
