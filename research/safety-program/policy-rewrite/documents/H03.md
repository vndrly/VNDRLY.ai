# Permit-to-work and simultaneous operations

Activities that can affect one another are coordinated so one crew does not defeat another crew's safeguards. This policy applies to overlapping work and to tasks subject to a legal or applicable site permit system.

The supervisor identifies nearby activities, shared equipment and boundaries that could change the task's safety. Crews agree the timing, separation and communication needed for those interfaces. For permitted work, the people issuing and performing the work confirm the scope, location, hazards and required precautions before starting. A permit records the arrangement; the actual isolation, ventilation, fire precautions or barriers must also be in place.

Workers stay within the agreed scope and communicate changes. If another activity introduces a hazard, or a permit condition is no longer met, the affected work pauses while the crews resolve the conflict. Work resumes within the applicable permit conditions and safe arrangement.

At handback, the crew tells the person controlling the equipment what work is complete and what condition remains.
