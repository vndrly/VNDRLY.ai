# Pressure pumping and testing

Pressure operations are arranged to prevent injury from released fluid, failed components and moving pipe or hose. This policy applies to pressure pumping, pressure testing and similar work.

The person directing the operation establishes the intended pressure, test medium and equipment limits. Components and connections are compatible with those conditions. Relief devices and pressure indications are suitable for the system. People are positioned away from likely release paths, unsupported components and connections that could whip or separate.

Pressure is increased in a controlled manner while the operator watches for leaks, movement and abnormal indications. No one tightens, disconnects or repairs a pressurized connection. A leak is not located by placing a hand in the release path. The system is depressurized and its safe condition confirmed before adjustment or opening.

If equipment moves, pressure behaves unexpectedly or containment fails, the operator stops and releases pressure safely. Access remains restricted until the danger is controlled. Specific test procedures and equipment limits come from the actual system and governing equipment requirements, not a universal pressure value or fixed exclusion distance.
