# Excavation and buried services

Excavation is planned to prevent cave-ins, strikes on buried services and falls into the work area. This policy applies to digging, trenching and ground disturbance.

Before excavation, the responsible person locates underground utilities using the required locating process and establishes their position as the work approaches them. Equipment, spoil and traffic are positioned so they do not undermine the edge or expose people to falling material. Workers stay clear of excavation equipment and suspended loads.

For construction excavations, the competent person performs the inspections required by 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P, including daily inspections before work and after conditions that increase the hazard. Protective systems, access and egress, atmospheric precautions and water controls are provided when the standard's conditions trigger them. Employees do not enter an excavation showing signs of failure while those conditions remain.

If a utility is damaged, soil moves, water enters or an atmospheric hazard develops, people leave the affected area and notify the supervisor. Work resumes only after the hazard has been addressed. Depth alone does not establish that a trench is safe.
