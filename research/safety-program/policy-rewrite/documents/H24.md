# Specialist operations

Specialist work is planned around its actual hazards and governing rules. This policy applies only when the company performs or supports radiation work, explosives operations, diving, marine work or rail activity.

The manager identifies the specialist activity before assigning it. The people arranging the work establish which employer or operating authority controls the operation and how nearby workers will be kept clear. Equipment, access and communication are selected for the specific environment. Support staff receive the information needed to avoid entering the specialist work area or interfering with its controls.

Licenses, qualifications, medical provisions and operating arrangements are applied when the law governing that activity requires them. Radiation and explosives work use the relevant licensed arrangements; commercial diving follows its applicable diving standard; marine and rail activities follow the rules governing their location and operation. These duties are not assigned to companies that do not perform or encounter the activity.

An unexpected exposure, lost boundary or change beyond the planned activity prompts withdrawal and contact with the person directing the specialist operation. Untrained coworkers do not improvise a rescue or handle unfamiliar specialist equipment.
