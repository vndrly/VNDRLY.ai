# Personal protective equipment

Protective equipment is selected for the hazards that remain in the work. This policy applies to company tasks requiring eye, face, head, hand, foot, body, hearing or respiratory protection.

The employer assesses workplace hazards and selects suitable protective equipment. Where 29 CFR 1910.132 applies, the hazard-assessment certification and employee training required by that standard are provided. Workers understand when protection is needed, how to put it on, its limitations and how to care for it. The equipment fits the worker and can be used with other protection without defeating either item.

Workers check their equipment and replace or report damaged items. Contaminated protection is handled so the hazard is not transferred to vehicles, eating areas or other people. Required equipment is available before the worker enters the exposure.

If protective equipment cannot be worn safely or does not control the exposure, the worker tells the supervisor and the task is adjusted. Respiratory protection, hearing conservation and other specialist programs apply when their own triggers are met.
