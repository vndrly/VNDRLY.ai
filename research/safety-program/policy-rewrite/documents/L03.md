# Stop work and safe restart

Anyone who identifies an immediate danger can stop the affected activity and move to a safe position. This policy applies to company work and to hazards created by work taking place nearby.

The person raising the concern tells the people exposed and the supervisor what was observed. The supervisor makes the area safe, listens to the concern, and addresses the condition with the people who understand the equipment or activity. A stop should cover the work exposed to the hazard; unrelated work can continue when it remains safe.

Work restarts after the hazard has been removed or controlled and the affected people understand the safe method. Where the activity has an applicable legal permit, isolation or entry condition, that condition is restored before the activity resumes. A supervisor does not direct a worker to bypass a safeguard to recover lost time.

No one is penalized for a good-faith stop or for reporting a hazard. If the parties disagree about safety, the matter goes to a manager who can resolve the hazard before the affected work proceeds.
