# Lifting, rigging and suspended loads

Lifting equipment and rigging are selected and used within their capacity for the actual load and arrangement. This policy applies to cranes, hoists and rigged loads.

The people planning the lift establish the load's weight, balance, attachment points and intended path. Rigging is suitable for the load, hitch and working conditions. Damaged or unidentified gear is not used where its capacity or condition cannot be established. The operator considers equipment configuration, ground support and obstructions before lifting.

People remain outside the fall and crush path of a load. No one rides a suspended load or passes beneath it. The operator follows the agreed signals and stops if a signal is unclear or the load moves unexpectedly. Tag lines are used only where they can be handled without putting a person in the load path or creating an entanglement hazard.

Crane operators, riggers and signal persons meet the requirements applicable to their equipment and work. Construction crane certification and signal-person qualification are applied where the construction crane standard requires them, rather than to every worker near a lift.
