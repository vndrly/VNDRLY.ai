# Leadership commitment and safety responsibilities

The company supports safe work by providing suitable equipment, understandable instructions and time to address hazards. This policy applies to company operations and the people directing them.

Managers make safety responsibilities clear when allocating work. Supervisors explain the task, arrange the people and equipment needed to perform it safely, and respond to concerns raised by workers. Workers follow the safety instructions relevant to their work and tell their supervisor when conditions or equipment make those instructions unsafe or impractical.

Production targets do not justify exposing people to an uncontrolled serious hazard. When a concern cannot be resolved at the work area, the supervisor brings it to the manager able to provide a solution. Work affected by the hazard remains stopped while that solution is put in place.

Management uses information from workers, incidents and equipment problems to correct conditions that could cause harm. Raising a safety concern or reporting an injury in good faith does not result in retaliation.
