# Driving and fatigue

Company driving is arranged so the driver can remain alert and operate the vehicle safely. This policy applies to travel performed for company work.

Drivers use the correct license for the vehicle, wear seat belts, obey traffic laws and secure the vehicle's load. They check visibility, tires, lights and other conditions relevant to safe travel and report defects. Phones or other distractions are not used in a way that takes attention from driving. Speed is adjusted for traffic, road surface, weather and visibility rather than relying only on the posted limit.

The person arranging travel considers journey length, working time, rest and the availability of a safe stopping place. A fatigued driver stops in a safe location and tells the supervisor when the trip cannot be completed safely. Schedule pressure does not justify continuing while impaired or sleepy.

Regulated commercial driving follows the applicable hours-of-service, qualification and inspection rules. Those rules are not imposed on unrelated workers or vehicles outside their scope. After a collision or roadside breakdown, people protect themselves from traffic, call for help and avoid unsafe recovery attempts.
