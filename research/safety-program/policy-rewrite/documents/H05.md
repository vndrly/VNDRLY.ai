# Confined-space entry

The company identifies spaces with restricted entry or exit and evaluates the hazards before people enter them. Employees do not enter a space simply because it appears empty or because someone else entered it previously.

For a permit-required space, the employer uses the entry program and permit required by the applicable confined-space standard. The entry arrangement addresses isolation, atmospheric testing, ventilation, communication, the attendant's duties and rescue. Entrants understand the hazards and leave when an alarm, prohibited condition or instruction to evacuate occurs. Entry supervisors confirm entry conditions and end entry when those conditions fail.

Atmospheric testing uses suitable equipment and considers oxygen, flammable atmospheres and toxic contaminants. Ventilation does not replace isolation of sources that could engulf, energize or contaminate the space. Rescue is arranged before entry; an unprotected coworker does not enter to attempt an improvised rescue.

General-industry permit entries follow 29 CFR 1910.146, including required training and rescue provisions. Construction entries follow the construction confined-space rules. Alternative procedures or reclassification are used only when their regulatory conditions are met.
