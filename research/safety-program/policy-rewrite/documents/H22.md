# Manual handling and ergonomic work

Work is arranged to reduce strain, awkward posture and uncontrolled movement of loads. This policy applies to lifting, carrying, pushing, pulling and repetitive tasks.

Before moving a load, the worker considers its weight, shape, grip and route. Mechanical aids, smaller loads or assistance are used where needed to avoid losing control or excessive strain. The route is cleared and the destination prepared so a load does not have to be held while space is made. Hands are kept away from pinch points when setting material down.

Workstations and tools are adjusted to reduce prolonged reaching, twisting and force. Repetitive tasks can be varied or paused when the work allows, particularly when fatigue affects control. A worker does not attempt an unfamiliar heavy lift solely because another person previously moved the item.

Pain, tingling, weakness or a task that cannot be performed comfortably is raised with the supervisor so the method can be changed.
