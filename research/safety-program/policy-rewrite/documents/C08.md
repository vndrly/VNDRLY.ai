# First aid and emergency response roles

Emergency coverage is arranged for the work location and the help that can reach it. This policy applies to occupational first aid and designated response duties.

The employer determines how workers will obtain prompt medical attention. Where an infirmary, clinic or hospital is not in near proximity, the first-aid training and supply provisions of the applicable OSHA medical and first-aid standard are met. Emergency contact information and the means to describe the work location are accessible to the people who may need them.

Designated responders receive the preparation required for their assigned response. Employees who only evacuate receive evacuation instructions rather than being assigned specialist firefighting, hazardous-material or rescue duties. Employees with occupational exposure to blood or other potentially infectious material are covered by the applicable bloodborne-pathogens protections.

At an emergency, workers raise the alarm, summon help and act within their training while avoiding further exposure. They do not enter an unsafe atmosphere or energized area to reach a casualty. After help arrives, the supervisor communicates relevant hazard information and protects private medical details.
