# Understanding incident causes

The company examines incidents to understand what failed and prevent recurrence. This policy applies to injuries, significant near misses and other events selected for examination based on what happened.

Medical care and making the area safe come first. The supervisor gathers the relevant facts from the people, equipment and conditions involved. Questions address what the work required, what information and safeguards were available, and how the situation developed. Workers can explain their actions without being pressured to agree with an assumed cause.

The examination separates observations from conclusions. A person's last action is considered alongside equipment condition, task design, communication and competing work. The company chooses corrections that address the causes identified rather than relying only on a reminder to be careful.

The scope of the examination matches the event; Where a legally covered process incident triggers the investigation duties in OSHA process safety management, those specific duties apply. Relevant findings are shared with people whose work could be affected, with private information protected.
