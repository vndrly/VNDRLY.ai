# Opening process equipment and handback

Piping, vessels and other process equipment are opened only after their contents and stored energy have been controlled. This policy applies to disconnecting, draining, opening or removing components that may contain pressure or hazardous material.

The crew identifies the correct equipment and communicates with the person controlling the process. Isolation addresses all connected sources. Pressure is relieved, contents managed and residual hazards checked before the first opening. Workers use a position and method that keep them clear of a possible release, with protective equipment suited to the material.

An unexpected pressure indication, blocked drain, leak or unidentified connection is treated as a reason to pause and resolve the condition. Loosening a connection is not used as the initial means of proving equipment depressurized when a safer verification method is available.

After work, the crew tells the process controller which components were changed, what remains open or isolated, and whether the equipment is ready for restoration. Restoration follows the applicable isolation and operating procedure. The area is checked for leaks and exposed people are clear before the process returns to service.
