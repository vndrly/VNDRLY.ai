# Vacuum and high-pressure cleaning equipment

Industrial cleaning equipment is operated to control pressure, suction, hazardous contents and movement of hose. This policy applies to vacuum trucks, hydroblasting and related cleaning systems.

The supervisor identifies the material being removed, the receiving capacity and any flammable, toxic or reactive hazard. Hoses, fittings and nozzles suit the pressure, vacuum and material. The crew controls hose movement and positions people away from the jet, suction opening and possible release paths. Deadman controls and other safety devices remain functional.

No one directs a pressure jet toward a person, reaches into a live suction line or clears a blockage with the system energized. The supply is stopped and pressure or vacuum safely released before opening or adjustment. Equipment is grounded or bonded where required for the actual flammable-material operation.
