# Rescue arrangements

Work with a foreseeable need for assisted rescue is arranged so help can reach a person without exposing unprotected coworkers. This policy applies to confined-space entry, fall-arrest work and other tasks with a rescue hazard.

The supervisor considers how an incapacitated person could be reached and removed, the location's access and the equipment needed. Calling emergency services is considered in light of the response the task needs, rather than assumed to solve every rescue problem. Workers understand how to summon that help and keep the access route available.

For permit-space work, the employer meets the rescue-service evaluation, access and practice provisions of the applicable confined-space rule. For personal fall-arrest work, prompt rescue or self-rescue is provided as required by the governing fall-protection rule. These task-specific obligations are not imposed on staff without those exposures.

During an emergency, workers raise the alarm and keep clear of the hazard unless prepared and assigned to the rescue involved. A failed rescue arrangement is addressed before the exposed task continues.
