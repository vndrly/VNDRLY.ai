# Equipment maintenance

Equipment is kept in a condition suitable for the work it performs. This policy applies to company-owned, rented and hired equipment used in operations.

The person arranging equipment identifies the machine, its intended use and the operating limits supplied with it. Maintenance follows the manufacturer's instructions and any equipment-specific legal requirements. The supervisor arranges access to operating and maintenance information for the people who use or service the equipment. An asset list may help manage equipment, but

Operators report unusual noise, leakage, overheating, damage or a failed safety device. Equipment with a defect affecting safety is not used until the defect is corrected. Repairs are performed with hazardous energy controlled and with replacement parts suited to the equipment.

Before return to use, the person responsible for the repair confirms that the relevant guards and safety functions are restored. Statutory inspections and maintenance records are retained where the rule for the equipment requires them.
