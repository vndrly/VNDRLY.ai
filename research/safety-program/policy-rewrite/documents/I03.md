# Correcting unsafe conditions

Unsafe conditions are corrected in a way that addresses the hazard people actually face. This policy applies to problems identified through work, reports, equipment faults and incidents.

The supervisor first protects anyone exposed. A permanent solution is selected with the people who understand the task. Removing the hazard or fixing equipment is preferred where practicable to adding warnings around a condition that can be eliminated. If a temporary measure is needed, the affected workers know how it protects them and where its limits are.

The person arranging the correction makes responsibility clear and follows up at a time appropriate to the risk. Work is not represented as safe merely because a task has been marked complete. Before affected work resumes, the crew confirms that the condition has been corrected or controlled for that work.

Workers report a correction that does not work or introduces a different hazard. The supervisor revises the solution rather than treating the first action as final.
