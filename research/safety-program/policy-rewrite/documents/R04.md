# Checking safety controls

Safety checks focus on whether the controls needed for the work are present and functioning. This policy applies to supervisors and workers looking at conditions in their own work areas.

A useful check follows the actual hazard: whether a guard prevents access, an isolation controls energy, a walkway separates pedestrians or a detector responds as intended. The person checking discusses the work with those doing it and avoids interrupting a safety-critical step. A completed checklist does not override an unsafe physical condition.

When a serious gap is found, exposed work pauses and the supervisor arranges a correction. A minor problem that can be safely corrected at once is addressed without creating an unnecessary approval process. Workers can point out limitations in a check or information that changes its conclusion.

Inspections and audits required by a specific regulation are performed within that regulation's scope and interval. Findings are used to fix the affected hazard and communicate the safe arrangement to people who rely on it.
