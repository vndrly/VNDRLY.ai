# Equipment checks and defects

Equipment users check the conditions that can affect safe operation before use. This policy applies to tools, vehicles and equipment used in company work.

The check is appropriate to the item: guards and leads on a power tool, visible damage and markings on lifting gear, or tires, controls and visibility on mobile equipment. A check is not a requirement to dismantle equipment or complete a generic checklist for every use. Equipment-specific legal examination and documentation requirements still apply where triggered.

If a defect could cause injury, the user stops using the item, keeps others from using it and informs the supervisor. Isolation, a tag, a barrier or removal from the work area can make its status clear, depending on the equipment. A warning label alone does not make a dangerous item safe to operate.

The supervisor arranges repair or replacement. The item returns to use after the defect has been corrected and the relevant safety functions checked. If the user cannot tell whether damage affects safe use, the equipment stays out of the affected operation while the question is resolved.
