# Mobile equipment and lifting roles

Equipment operators and people directing lifts receive preparation suited to the equipment and task. This policy applies to forklifts, mobile equipment, crane operators, riggers and signal persons within their assigned roles.

The supervisor matches the operator's training and experience to the machine, attachments and work area. Operators understand capacity, stability, visibility and separation from pedestrians. They stop when the route or load cannot be controlled safely and report defects before another person uses the equipment.

Powered industrial truck operators covered by 29 CFR 1910.178 receive formal instruction, practical training and evaluation by a person with the required knowledge, training and experience. Their performance is evaluated at least every three years, with refresher training and evaluation when the standard's triggers occur. Construction crane operator certification, employer evaluation and signal-person qualification apply where the relevant construction crane rules require them.

These practical and qualification duties apply to the covered equipment roles, not every course participant or employee near equipment. A worker who has not been prepared for a different machine or lifting role tells the supervisor before undertaking that work.
