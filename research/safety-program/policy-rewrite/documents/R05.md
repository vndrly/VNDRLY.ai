# Management decisions and improvement

Management uses information about company work to remove obstacles to safe operation. This policy applies to decisions about resources, priorities and changes in the company's safety arrangements.

Managers consider worker concerns, incidents, recurring defects and changes in services when deciding where improvement is needed. They ask whether people have suitable equipment, understandable instructions and enough support to perform the work safely. An improvement is chosen for the problem it addresses, rather than solely to increase a displayed score.

Responsibilities for a selected improvement are made clear. Managers provide the resources needed and communicate changes to the supervisors and workers affected. If a solution does not address the problem or creates another hazard, it is adjusted. Workers can raise unresolved concerns through another manager when the immediate supervisor cannot resolve them.

 Any review or documentation specifically required for a regulated activity remains tied to that activity. The company's ordinary management arrangements can be used to make and follow through on safety decisions.
