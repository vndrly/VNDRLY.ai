# Subcontractor selection and safety coordination

The company selects subcontractors for the work they will actually perform and coordinates shared hazards before that work begins. This policy applies when the company engages another business to perform part of its service.

The person arranging the subcontract explains the scope, location and hazards known to the company. The subcontractor explains its work method and any hazards it may introduce to other people. Together they identify who controls shared equipment, isolations, access and emergency communication. A short discussion can resolve a straightforward interface.

The subcontractor provides people and equipment suitable for its agreed work. When a specialist license or qualification is legally required for that work, the selection takes that requirement into account. A change in scope is discussed before the added activity exposes people to new hazards.

If work creates an uncontrolled danger, the affected activity stops while the companies resolve it. For work on a process actually covered by OSHA process safety management, the host and contract employers also perform their respective contractor duties under that regulation.
