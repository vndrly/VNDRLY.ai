# Service scope, legal duties and current instructions

Safety requirements follow the services the company performs, the work location and the hazards people encounter. This policy applies when selecting services, planning work or issuing instructions.

Managers describe the company's services accurately. Supervisors use instructions suited to the task and equipment in use. Workers ask for clarification when instructions conflict, are unreadable or describe a different task. An outdated instruction is replaced or corrected before it leads someone into an unsafe action.

Legal requirements are applied to the activity and jurisdiction that trigger them. A requirement for one kind of operation is not automatically imposed on every company, role or service. Company staff can obtain the current instructions and safety information needed for their work.

When a service, piece of equipment or work method changes, the supervisor checks whether the existing instructions still address its hazards. Conflicts affecting safety are resolved with the responsible company or site contact before the affected work continues.
