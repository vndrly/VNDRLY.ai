# Noise and hearing protection

Noise exposure is controlled to protect hearing and preserve the ability to communicate warnings. This policy applies to noisy equipment and work areas.

The supervisor considers the level and duration of exposure and arranges measurement when needed to determine whether hearing-conservation duties apply. Quieter equipment, maintenance, isolation and other feasible controls are considered before relying only on hearing protectors. Workers wear the selected protection correctly in designated noisy work and tell the supervisor when it prevents essential communication or does not fit.

For employees covered by 29 CFR 1910.95, the hearing-conservation program applies at the standard's action level and includes the required monitoring, audiometry, protectors and training. Construction noise requirements are applied to construction work.

Workers report ringing, muffled hearing or a sudden change after noise exposure and obtain appropriate medical attention. A missing protector or unexpected increase in noise is addressed before exposure continues. Hearing protection is selected for the actual noise, not simply because it has the highest advertised rating.
