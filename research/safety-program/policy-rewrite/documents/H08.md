# Work at height and dropped objects

Elevated work is arranged to prevent falls and protect people below. This policy applies to work near unprotected edges, openings, scaffolds and other elevated positions.

The supervisor selects safe access and a working surface suitable for the task. Guardrails, covers or other collective protection are used where appropriate. When personal fall protection is required, the system is suitable for the anchorage, fall distance and equipment involved. Workers inspect their equipment and keep connectors, harnesses and lifelines away from damaging edges and incompatible connections.

Ladders are placed on a stable surface, secured where needed and used without overreaching. Scaffolds are erected, inspected and used under the applicable scaffold rules. Tools and materials are kept from falling by secure storage, toe boards, tool retention or separation of the area below, as suited to the work.

Work stops when access, edge protection or fall protection becomes unsafe. Fall-height thresholds, training and rescue duties follow the standard governing the activity; construction and general-industry thresholds are not treated as interchangeable.
