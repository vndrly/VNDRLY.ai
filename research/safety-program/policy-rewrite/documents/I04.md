# Sharing useful safety learning

Safety learning is communicated to people whose work can benefit from it. This policy applies to lessons from incidents, near misses, changed equipment and successful improvements.

The supervisor shares the facts needed to recognize the hazard and use the improved work method. A useful message states what happened, the condition that mattered and the action relevant to the recipient's work. Personal medical information and unrelated identifying details are left out. An uncertain cause is described as uncertain rather than presented as an established fact.

Workers can ask how a lesson applies to their task and point out differences in equipment or conditions. A lesson from another operation is not automatically converted into a mandatory company rule. If it reveals an actual hazard in current work, the supervisor addresses that hazard with the affected crew.

A brief discussion after unusual work can identify what helped and what should change. Where the lesson changes an instruction people rely on, the current instruction is updated and explained to those affected.
