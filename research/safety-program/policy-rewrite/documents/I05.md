# Accurate safety and exposure information

Company safety information accurately represents the period, people and operations it describes. This policy applies to injury history, hours, exposure measurements and information supplied for safety reporting.

The person preparing information uses the underlying records and checks that totals refer to the same period and company scope. Estimates are identified as estimates. Missing information is not entered as zero, and a blank field is not described as proof that no incidents occurred. Corrections are made when an error is discovered rather than carried into a later report.

OSHA recordkeeping decisions use the applicable recording criteria and exemptions. An injury is not omitted to improve a rate or because a worker completed training. Exposure results identify the substance or hazard measured and the conditions represented by the sample; a result from one task is not assumed to cover every service.

Access to medical and personal information is limited to its legitimate use. Required records are kept for the periods set by the rule governing them.
