# Permit, isolation, gas testing and electrical duties

Safety-critical duties are allocated to people who understand the specific task and equipment. This policy applies to people issuing or receiving permits, applying isolations, testing atmospheres or performing electrical work.

The supervisor explains the person's duties, limits and communication interfaces. A permit issuer understands the activity and conditions being authorized. An isolation worker identifies energy sources and verifies the controlled condition. A gas tester understands the detector, sampling location and limits of the result. An electrical worker performs only tasks within the person's electrical qualification.

Role training follows the governing task standard. Permit-space training, energy-control training and qualified electrical work practices apply where the corresponding regulations cover the work. A detector reading or a course completion is used for its actual purpose and is not treated as authority to undertake a different specialist role.

Workers do not accept a reading, permit or isolation they cannot verify within their duties. They ask the supervisor or responsible specialist to resolve the gap.
