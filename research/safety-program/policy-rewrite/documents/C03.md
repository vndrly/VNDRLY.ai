# Support for new and changed-role workers

A person new to a task receives support appropriate to their experience and the hazards involved. This policy applies to new hires, transferred workers and experienced workers learning unfamiliar equipment or work methods.

The supervisor asks about relevant experience, explains the task and assigns work the person can safely perform. Instructions identify the hazards, safeguards and circumstances in which to stop and ask for help. An experienced coworker or supervisor remains available at a level suited to the task. Experience in one type of work is not assumed to cover a different machine or exposure.

As the person becomes familiar with the work, the supervisor adjusts support based on what the person can do safely. Questions and good-faith reports of uncertainty are encouraged.

When a task requires training, qualification or evaluation by law, that requirement is met before the person performs the regulated role. A mistake or unexpected condition prompts a safe pause, explanation and correction rather than pressure to continue beyond the person's capability.
