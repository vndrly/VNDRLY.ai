# Hot work and fire prevention

Welding, cutting and other ignition-producing work are performed where sparks, hot material and flame cannot ignite combustible material or a hazardous atmosphere. This policy applies to hot work in shops and at field locations.

The worker checks the surrounding area, including openings and the opposite side of walls or floors. Combustible material is moved away or protected. Containers and equipment that held flammable material are prepared for hot work before cutting or welding begins. Cylinders, hoses and electrical leads are kept in safe condition and positioned away from damage.

The person responsible for the area authorizes hot work under the applicable arrangement. A fire watch is provided where the conditions in the governing welding and cutting standard require one, with suitable extinguishing equipment and the required watch period. Hot-work permits are used where a governing rule or the site's permit system requires them.

Work stops if flammable vapors, uncontrolled sparks or changing conditions defeat fire precautions. Anyone discovering a fire raises the alarm, withdraws as needed and calls for help rather than attempting an unsafe firefighting action.
