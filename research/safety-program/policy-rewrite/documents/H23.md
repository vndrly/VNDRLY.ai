# Guards, tools and housekeeping

Tools and machinery are used in a condition that protects people from cutting, crushing, entanglement and flying material. This policy applies to shops, maintenance work and field tools.

Workers use the correct tool and compatible accessory for the task. Guards and safety devices remain in place during operation. Loose clothing, jewelry and hair are kept from rotating parts. Material is supported so hands do not enter a cutting or pinch zone. Tools with damaged handles, leads, guards or accessories are taken out of use until repaired or replaced.

Adjusting, clearing or servicing equipment is performed with hazardous energy controlled where the task exposes a person to unexpected movement. A jammed machine is not reached into while it can cycle. Compressed air, sharp scrap and cutting debris are handled without directing hazards toward other people.

Walkways, exits and access to controls remain clear. Spills and trip hazards are corrected or isolated promptly. A guard that interferes with the task is a reason to choose a suitable method or tool, not to bypass the guard.
