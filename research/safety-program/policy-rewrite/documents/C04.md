# Training delivery and completion

Company training gives workers usable instruction for the work they perform. This policy applies to courses assigned through VNDRLY and other training selected by the company.

Training materials explain the subject, demonstrate decisions through examples and provide questions that check understanding. The administrator uses TRAINING to review the materials and ASSIGN to send a course to the intended people, groups or positions. Required new-hire and role-change assignments are configured on the course Policy page.

Learners answer the knowledge checks. When an answer is incorrect, the learner reviews the explanation and acknowledges the correct answer through the course's remediation process. Original and remediated scores remain distinct. Completion is recorded when the course's stated completion conditions are met. A separate certification exam retains its own passing standard.

The administrator uses REVIEW for company training compliance. Training completion contributes to the applicable training metric, alongside the other elements of the scoring rubric. A practical evaluation is required only for tasks where the governing law requires it;
