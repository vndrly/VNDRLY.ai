# Medical help and return to work

People receive prompt assistance after injury or illness, and medical information is handled privately. This policy applies to work-related medical response and arrangements for resuming work.

Workers call for emergency help when needed and notify the supervisor without delaying care. The supervisor provides responders with the location and relevant hazards, including chemical information when exposure is suspected. First aid is given within the responder's training while avoiding additional danger.

The company receives only the medical information needed for its lawful responsibilities, such as work restrictions or a legally required exposure-surveillance opinion. Detailed diagnoses are not circulated to coworkers. Access, disclosure and retention follow the rules governing the particular medical or exposure record.

When a person returns with restrictions, the supervisor arranges duties within those restrictions and seeks clarification when the job cannot be performed as described. Medical evaluations are obtained when the actual work or governing rule requires them. A worker experiencing worsening symptoms is helped to obtain care rather than pressured to complete an assignment.
