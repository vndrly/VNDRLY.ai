# Silica, asbestos, lead and coating exposure

Work that can disturb hazardous dusts, fibers or coatings is identified before material is cut, blasted, demolished or removed. This policy applies to affected construction, maintenance, surface preparation and abatement tasks.

The supervisor determines what material is present and how the task may expose people. Workers do not assume an older coating, insulation or building material is harmless because it looks familiar. The work method uses appropriate containment, dust collection, wet methods or other controls for the material and activity. Dry sweeping and compressed-air cleaning are avoided where prohibited or where they spread hazardous dust.

The employer applies the substance-specific standard that actually governs the work. Silica exposure-control plans, asbestos training and regulated areas, lead monitoring and medical surveillance are provided when the respective regulatory conditions require them. Required respirator use follows the respiratory-protection program.

Unexpected suspect material or failed dust control prompts a pause in the affected operation. Contaminated material is contained and handled without exposing nearby people or carrying contamination into eating areas, vehicles or homes. Material identification and exposure results determine the next safe method.
