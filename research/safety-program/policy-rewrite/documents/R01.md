# Emergency action and site coordination

Workers know how to raise an alarm, leave danger and obtain help at their work location. This policy applies to emergencies affecting company personnel.

The person directing the work explains the alarm method, emergency contact, evacuation route and assembly location. At a shared site, the company uses the site's relevant emergency information and clarifies how its people will be accounted for. Routes and assembly locations avoid the hazard rather than simply following the shortest path.

On an alarm or discovery of an emergency, workers stop safely if time permits, warn others and move to the designated safe location. They tell the responsible person about missing coworkers or information responders need. They do not reenter the danger area to collect belongings or search without an appropriate response role.

Where 29 CFR 1910.38 requires an emergency action plan, the employer provides its required elements and employee review; the standard permits oral communication of the plan for employers with ten or fewer employees. Other applicable emergency rules are followed for their specific work.
