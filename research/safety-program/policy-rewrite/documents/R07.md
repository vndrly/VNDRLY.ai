# Spills and environmental releases

Releases are handled to protect people first and limit the spread of material where it can be done safely. This policy applies to fuels, chemicals, process fluids and waste handled in company work.

Workers identify the material from safe information such as the label or safety data sheet. They warn others, keep people away and report the release to the supervisor or site contact. Ignition sources are avoided when flammable material may be present. A valve or source is stopped only when the worker can do so without entering danger.

A small incidental spill can be handled by personnel prepared for that material using suitable protection and equipment. An unknown, uncontrolled or dangerous release is left to the appropriate emergency responders. Employees are not assigned hazardous-material emergency response merely because they have taken a general spill-awareness course.

The responsible manager makes legally required release notifications and arranges suitable waste handling. Where the operation triggers OSHA hazardous-waste or emergency-response duties, those role-specific requirements apply. Workers report exposure or symptoms promptly and do not wash a release into a drain or waterway as an improvised cleanup method.
