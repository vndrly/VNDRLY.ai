# Training matched to work roles

Training is assigned according to the services a company provides and the tasks each person performs. This policy applies to company personnel, including new hires and people changing roles.

The administrator selects the relevant positions and people on the course Policy page and sets the intended new-hire or role-change rule. Supervisors explain the duties of a changed role and identify training needed for hazards the person will encounter. A person performing one service is not assigned unrelated specialist training solely because it exists in the Library.

VNDRLY-required training follows the applicable service and role bundle. Additional vendor-selected training can support company work without becoming a scored VNDRLY requirement. Administrators use REVIEW to see who has or has not completed the relevant training, with available scores retained in the record.

Where a task has a legal qualification or evaluation requirement, the employer applies it to that task and role. Missing task training is addressed before the person performs work for which that training is required.
