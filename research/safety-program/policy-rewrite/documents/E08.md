# Safe shops and facilities

Company premises provide safe access, usable emergency exits and work areas suited to their activities. This policy applies to shops, yards, stores and other company facilities.

The person managing the area keeps walkways, exits, electrical panels and emergency equipment accessible. Materials are stored securely without unstable stacks or blocked routes. Lighting and ventilation are suitable for the work. Flammable materials and incompatible chemicals are stored under the requirements applicable to those substances.

Workers return tools and materials to safe locations, manage spills and scrap, and report damaged floors, exposed wiring or failed guards. Machinery is positioned so its operating space does not force people into traffic or moving parts. Charging, welding and similar activities are arranged so their ignition and ventilation hazards are controlled.

An immediate hazard is isolated or the affected area is closed while it is corrected. Fire and medical access remains available during deliveries and temporary work. Equipment-specific examinations and legally required fire provisions apply where relevant;
