# Task hazard assessment

Work is planned with attention to the hazards of the actual task, its surroundings and other people who may be affected. This policy applies to routine work, unfamiliar tasks and changes during work.

Before starting, the people doing the work consider the main steps, sources of energy, movements, substances and environmental conditions that could cause harm. They choose controls that remove the hazard where practicable, then use suitable equipment, separation, work methods and protective equipment for remaining exposure. The supervisor resolves hazards the crew cannot control itself.

A familiar low-risk task may be addressed through a brief conversation and existing instructions. A written job hazard analysis is used when an applicable requirement calls for it or the company chooses it for the task;

Workers pause when the scope, equipment, people or conditions change in a way that defeats the planned controls. They adjust the method with the supervisor before resuming. If a safeguard cannot be established, the affected activity does not proceed.
