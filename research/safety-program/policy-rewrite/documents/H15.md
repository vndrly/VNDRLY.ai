# Respiratory protection

Respirators are selected for the contaminant, concentration and work conditions when engineering or other controls do not adequately protect employees. Required respirator use is managed under the applicable respiratory-protection program.

For work covered by 29 CFR 1910.134, the employer provides a suitable written program and a qualified program administrator. Required users receive a medical evaluation before fit testing or use. Tight-fitting respirators are fit tested before initial use, when the facepiece changes and at least annually, with additional testing when physical changes could affect fit. Users receive the required training and inspect and care for the respirator.

Workers perform a user seal check each time a tight-fitting respirator is put on. Facial hair or other conditions must not interfere with its seal or valve function. Cartridges follow the selected change schedule or appropriate service-life indicator, rather than odor alone.

If breathing resistance, leakage or equipment failure occurs, the worker leaves the hazardous area before removing the respirator. Voluntary use is handled under the standard's voluntary-use provisions, including the filtering-facepiece exception; required-use duties are not automatically applied to every dust-mask user.
