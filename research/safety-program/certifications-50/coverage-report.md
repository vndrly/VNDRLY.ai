# Oil and gas certification curriculum coverage

50 commonly encountered oil-and-gas credential and training pathways; not a statistically established prevalence ranking. Training programs, access credentials, licenses and professional certifications are explicitly distinguished.

**Status: curriculum development, not 50 finished or externally recognized certifications.**

Existing curriculum: 117 courses. Newly authored knowledge modules: 2.
The existing G99 private exam is not copied, modified or exposed by this build.
No existing assignment, completion, policy or safety score is changed.

Audience: Electrical workers and others exposed to electrical hazards

- Shock and arc-flash hazard recognition
- Electrically safe work condition
- Qualification and task limits
- Approach boundaries and protective measures
- Test instruments and verification
- Emergency release and response

Supporting courses: H12, G60, G67, H04, E07

Apply the governing OSHA scope and employer electrical-safety program. A generic online course does not make someone a qualified person for all equipment or confer an electrical license.

Renewal: Review qualification and retraining under the applicable program and adopted standard edition.

Coverage still to establish:
- Detailed adopted NFPA 70E edition crosswalk and equipment-specific demonstrated skills need review.
