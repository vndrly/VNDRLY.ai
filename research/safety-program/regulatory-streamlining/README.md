# Regulatory-only training requirements

This revision removes VNDRLY-added blanket practical assessments, assessor checklists and evidence-upload demands from the same 117 course families. It retains the actual instructional lessons, scenarios, knowledge checks and separate Energy Safety Core examination.

Energy Safety Core has eleven required learning sections: a short introduction and ten teaching chapters. Its benchmark and regulatory registers are optional references. Independent program review and partner acceptance remain VNDRLY management matters, not learner completion steps or an assertion of external accreditation.

`scripts/lib/streamlined-training.mjs` lists the retained regulatory duties with exact citations, actual covered roles and primary sources. A forklift operator evaluation is not assigned to every lifting worker; a respirator fit test is not assigned to everyone taking H2S awareness. Employer records required by an applicable regulation are distinct from a VNDRLY upload requirement. No generic outside assessor or independent practical certification is added.

Current teaching is generated by `scripts/build-task-training-release.mjs`. Original authoring artifacts remain historical inputs; the builder removes the superseded additions before publication. The read projection also removes those additions from authenticated original-version Library and assignment views without changing stored completion history or grading keys. Company-authored modifications are excluded from that projection.

`previous-course-identities.json` identifies the exact previously approved source/content hashes. Compatible completed versions retain company training credit and earned Energy Safety Core credentials; copied source labels with edited content do not. Publication adds current versions in the same series, carries existing training policies, and does not create repeat assignments, change bundles or overwrite historical templates/results.

`scripts/publish-streamlined-training.mjs --validate-only` checks the complete publication without database access. Its default production preflight is read-only. `--apply` publishes in guarded company transactions and verifies content, policy carry and preservation of prior rows. Deployment and live verification are separate evidence, not asserted by this source document.
