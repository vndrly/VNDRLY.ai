# C — Fleet Compliance and Commercial Authority Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Track applicable Federal/Oklahoma/Texas carrier, vehicle, insurance and driver requirements with private reviewed evidence, green/red assessments, nonblocking initial warnings and immutable partner disclosures.

**Architecture:** A pure versioned rules/evidence evaluator feeds the existing Fleet action services. Keep commercial authority, document assessment, actual operational holds and future explicit enforcement separate. Add narrow Fleet treatment for proven insurance-only approval lapses instead of weakening shared approval filters.

**Tech Stack:** Existing Zod/TypeScript/PostgreSQL/private storage/notifications and branded web/Expo components; no regulator or insurance provider integration is implied.

**Spec:** [Researched scope](../../fleet-operations-scope-ok-tx-2026-10-16.md), sections 3–6, 10 and 12; [master plan](2026-10-16-fleet-commercial-operations.md).

## Global Constraints

All master constraints apply. Initial `warn_only` applies at every canonical action and offline reconciliation checkpoint; document expiry/revocation/missing verification does not become a hidden access filter. Deliberate safety/maintenance stop-work controls stay authoritative. Red reasons distinguish confirmed failure from unknown/unverified evidence; acknowledgement never changes a red assessment to green. Private medical/testing documents and full license data remain reviewer-only. Source dates, exceptions and exact applicability are required; do not turn example legal thresholds into universal requirements.

## Review Focus

October 16 scope clarification: drivers belong to the company's available pool and join reusable Details; they are not assigned to sites. Qualification onboarding and assigned-activity access must work without driver site grants. Assess the saved Run transport order's Origin/Destination/cargo/time scope for each dispatched activity while preserving company/dispatcher commercial authority and immutable activity checkpoints.

- `auto_unapproved` may come from insurance expiry or a real commercial revocation: only provable compliance-only lapses retain scoped Fleet access.
- Company history lacks enough evidence of approval: do not invent an approval or delete performed work; reconcile it.
- Certificate upload is not policy/filing verification; expired/excluded coverage cannot become green because a COI exists.
- Renewal cannot rewrite prior red dispatch/start/closeout snapshots.
- Policy changes while a driver is offline: preserve capture-time decisions and apply explicit prospective enforcement rules, never backdate away work.
