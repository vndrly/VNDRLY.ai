# Work Hub expansion: approved scope

The September 9 interview and explicit unattended implementation/full-ship authorization supersede the earlier release timing. Publish tonight after verification; unconfigured payment, payroll tax/direct-deposit, and audio infrastructure must never simulate successful external execution. Screenshots are layout references only; do not copy their business data or image files into this repository.

## Workspace
Replicate the supplied collaboration workspace interactions using VNDRLY branding, light/dark themes and existing flat icons. Navigation: Activity, Chat, Crews & Channels, Calendar, Calls, Files & Notes, Tasks & Forms, Meetings, Billing & Payroll, AskV (text/voice), Import & Export, Administration. Users pin/reorder items with overflow under More. No Microsoft branding, domain setup, webinars, marketplace, video, screen sharing or external telephone calls.

## Collaboration
Collaboration Crews are separate from field assignment crews. Only company administrators create Crews. Designated owners manage members/channels. Channels are Crew-wide, private or explicitly shared across authorized relationships. Cross-company direct chats/calls require accepted invitations. Support conversation lists, favorites, drafts, unread filters, mentions, threads, reactions, author edit/delete with indicators and restricted audit history, attachment previews, notes and shared content. Only owners/admins assign tasks and publish announcements; urgent announcements notify their authorized audience. Audio calls and meetings with private voicemail and transcripts; recording is host-initiated with participant notice. No provider is presumed configured.

## Files, calendar and meetings
Personal and shared files, favorites, previews, replacement versions and recycle bin; editable native notes but no Office editing. Public file links require owners/admins, expiration and revocation and expose only the shared file. Calendar includes personal/Crew/project meetings, shifts, ticket schedule and deadlines with filters and day/week/month views. Meetings incorporates the reference scheduling-page layout and meeting types/availability; scheduling requires sign-in. Microsoft migration is an optional administrator-initiated one-time import with preview, mappings, retries, duplicate prevention and history, never writeback.

## Finance
Billing Manager may be delegated to office employees; company admins have billing access by default. Only admins refund. Prepare ticket-based and manual invoices for onboarded or external customers; save/issue/email/print/PDF/CSV, partial payments/deposits, public invoice viewing/payment links, card and ACH connection points, and record outside cash/check/bank payments without a platform fee. VNDRLY percentage fee is deducted from issuer proceeds; evaluation default 0.5%, configurable with optional cap. Refund platform fees proportionally. Preserve monetary precision, history and duplicate protection.

US W-2 only, independent employer payroll for every vendor/partner. Explicit Payroll Viewer/Preparer/Approver assignments by any company admin; admin alone never implies payroll-data access. Prepare/save/approve payroll, reports, employee self-service pay statements/W-2s and secure bank/tax forms when configured. Direct deposit, taxes and filings require real integration results. Do not store raw bank/SSN data as ordinary application fields. AskV respects payroll permissions and cannot authorize money movement.
