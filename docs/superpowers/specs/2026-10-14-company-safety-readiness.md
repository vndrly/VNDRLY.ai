# Company safety readiness

Approved intent: replace personal actions with organization-wide prevention and follow-up. User selected 60% readiness / 40% incident performance on October 14, 2026.

Use existing scoped company membership authority and data. Readiness categories are required safety training, explicitly safety-related library requirements, credentials, driver qualifications, inspections, and verified corrective actions. Each category contributes up to 10 points; incident performance contributes up to 40. Unknown evidence earns no claimed points and makes the result provisional, with possible-score range and coverage shown. Empty tables never prove compliance. This is an internal management indicator, not regulatory clearance.

Library authors explicitly mark safety-related requirements; published content versions remain immutable. Only saved completed/current-version responses earn points, with overdue refreshers outstanding. Do not count assignments as completed, polls, drafts, cancelled assignments, or old template versions. Training completion must be at or after the active module publication/version date. Credential records require verification and current expiry; absent qualification requirements remain unknown. Driver qualification coverage remains unknown until backed by canonical requirement records, never inferred from a job title.

Company members see aggregate counts only; management actions preserve existing destination permissions. Company identity comes from authenticated membership, never arbitrary query IDs. No new dependencies, no destructive migrations. Preserve existing incident metric as incidentScore while displaying composite readiness separately. Web, iOS and AskV consume the same server calculation.
